# FACTUAL-EDITING NO-GO — false-negative RED-TEAM (3 lenses)

## head-localization — NEGATIVE-IS-FRAGILE / MAJOR
**Strongest:** I ran the missing decisive test (all-heads/all-layers oracle edge-cut, gemma-2-2b-it, same hi/mid/lo CounterFact bands as the diag; /tmp/fe_res/fra_org_factedit/results/allheads/allheads.json). Sanity calibrated perfectly: Paris (P=0.20) R_top3=0.33 -> R_top8=0.79 -> R_allheads=0.99, so the all-heads machinery is sensitive. The negative's SPECIFIC target survives: on strongly-recalled facts (P>0.6) cutting the last-subject-token -> last edge is redundant at EVERY localization scale -- top3 median R=0.04, top8=0.07 (frac>=0.5 = 0), all-208-heads-last-subj=0.04. So localization paths (a) mislocalized and (b) too-few-heads are BOTH ruled out for the cell the FRA edit actually manipulates. BUT the negative's GENERALIZED claim ('subject->object attention transport is redundant/saturated; attention is non-load-bearing; hence ROME-must-edit-MLP') is overstated: zeroing the ENTIRE subject token-span -> last at all heads removes the fact on 7/12 strongly-recalled facts (clean, in-range, 0 artifacts in the hi band; median R=0.60, several at 0.95-0.999). Every hi-band subject is multi-token; the load-bearing attention lives in NON-last subject tokens distributed across many heads -- a region the last-subject-token SAE cell-edit structurally cannot reach. So there IS a load-bearing attention pathway on recalled facts; the FRA edit just isn't aimed at it.

**Next test:** Re-run the FRA cell-edit keying on the FULL subject token-span (not just the last subject token), aggregated over the top-8 (not top-3) extraction heads, at faithful scale c=1-2, on the 12 hi-band recalled facts -- and measure whether it (i) drops P(target) >=50% and (ii) keeps same-relation/different-subject collateral <15%. The all-heads subject-span oracle (median R=0.60, frac>=0.5=0.58 on hi) shows the on-target headroom now exists; the open question my test could not settle is whether that headroom is SURGICALLY reachable by the SAE feature-pair edit (vs. only by brute-force column-zeroing, which is off-distribution -- note the negative R artifacts in the lo band). If the span-keyed FRA edit clears both gates, the flagship is a true false-negative and ROME selectivity should run; if it cannot, the operational NO-GO stands.

**Bottom line:** The OPERATIONAL no-go is robust: the FRA cell-edit as specified (last-subject-token x relation cell at top-3 heads) genuinely cannot move strongly-recalled facts, and this is NOT a top-3 localization artifact -- expanding to 8 heads and even all 208 heads on that edge recovers nothing (hi-band R stays ~0.04-0.07, frac>=0.5=0), while the machinery is proven sensitive by the Paris sanity (R_allheads=0.99). But the negative's mechanistic INTERPRETATION (attention transport is globally redundant -> only MLP carries recall) is fragile/overstated: a broader attention ablation (whole subject span x all heads) removes the fact on the majority of recalled facts (clean hi-band median R=0.60), so a load-bearing, distributed, non-last-token attention pathway exists and the specified FRA edit simply doesn't target it -- a real edit-completeness gap, not proven redundancy.

## regime-and-model — NEGATIVE-IS-ROBUST / MINOR
**Strongest:** The recall-strength gating of the ORACLE edge-cut R survives WITHIN-relation, which defuses the "it's a relation-type / lexical-shortcut artifact" attack that my lens predicted would sink it. Across the diag bands, the same relation editorialized at different recall strengths shows R collapsing as P(target) rises: P37 (official language) lo P0.05-0.06 -> R 0.27-0.42, mid P0.19 -> R 0.71, hi P0.83 -> R 0.16; P30 (continent) mid P0.22 -> R 0.41, hi P0.91-0.93 -> R 0.01-0.09; P176 (manufacturer) mid P0.19 -> R 0.20, hi P0.77-0.96 -> R 0.01-0.13. So "attention edge non-load-bearing on well-recalled facts" is not merely the hi-band being saturated with lexically-predictable manufacturer facts (Nokia C7->Nokia, Honda CRF->Honda) where the object is trivially subject-cued — it holds even when relation is fixed. Band aggregates: hi best-R median 0.073, frac(>=0.5)=0.0 (0/12); lo/mid median 0.32/0.39, frac>=0.5 = 0.25/0.33. The sanity check is also internally honest: the screen's "R=+0.86 load-bearing" evidence came from a DIFFERENT model (base gemma-2-2b, bhhu4qdtg.output) at P(ans)=0.039 — a weak fact — and the diag correctly reproduces that as the lo-band regime (France on -it: P=0.20, R=0.32). For part (c): the screen ALREADY saw this coming — it demoted factual-recall D2 from 2 to 1 citing "probe CCF=0 + 31% MLP-extraction lit" and named D2 (object MLP-recomputed, the greater-than signature) as "exactly the §6.1 flagship falsifier," so the result is the pre-registered D2-fail, NOT a surprise falsification of the FRA thesis. FRA is correctly classified as the wrong tool for an MLP-resident behavior (literature-consistent: ROME/MEMIT edit MLP).

**Next test:** The ONE gap that would convert "robust" to "airtight" is part (b): the MLP-confound is ASSUMED, never demonstrated — neither factedit_precheck.py nor factedit_diag.py ablates the subject-position MLP; "the object is likely MLP-written by the subject position" is an inference from (attention-edge-non-load-bearing + ROME-consistency). Cheap test on the SAME 12 hi-band facts (one L4 pod, ~10 min, reuses the existing harness/HookedTransformer/cut machinery): logit-lens the residual stream for the target token at the SUBJECT position vs the LAST position across layers, AND zero/mean-ablate the subject-position MLP output (hook_mlp_out at the subject token, layer-swept) measuring R_mlp = 1 - P_cut/P_base. PREDICTION if negative is robust: on hi-band facts the target token is already high-rank in the residual at the subject position before the extraction heads fire, and subject-MLP ablation yields R_mlp >> the attention-edge R~0.07 (object is MLP-resident). If instead subject-MLP ablation ALSO gives R~0 and the object only materializes at the last position, the "MLP-resident" story is wrong and the negative is a localization/redundancy puzzle rather than a clean ROME-consistent MLP result — that would weaken (not flip) the NO-GO.

**Bottom line:** The factual-editing NO-GO is robust, not a false negative. The recall-strength gating of the oracle edge-cut holds within-relation (P37/P30/P176), so it is a genuine property of well-recalled facts in gemma-2-2b-it and not an artifact of head-localization, the SAE/edit mechanism, the metric, or a relation-type/lexical confound; the diag's oracle edge-cut (cutting the entire subject->last score, no SAE) is the correct strong control and it confirms there is no load-bearing attention edge to reach for. The only soft spot is scope/attribution, not validity: (a) it is single-model (gemma-2-2b-it only) so "fundamental property of factual recall" overclaims — a 9B/base re-run is untested though the within-relation evidence makes a regime-flip unlikely; (b) the "object is MLP-written at the subject position" mechanism is asserted, not measured (no subject-MLP ablation anywhere), and a one-pod logit-lens + subject-MLP-knockout would close it; and (c) the verdict is correctly the pre-registered §6.1 D2-fail the screen flagged when it demoted D2->1, i.e. "facts are MLP-stored, FRA controls attending not recall, use ROME" — it does not falsify the broader FRA thesis, it confirms the rubric's G-score/G-post boundary.

## salvage-and-metric — NEGATIVE-IS-FRAGILE / MAJOR
**Strongest:** The gate-(i) NO-GO ("0/28 facts reach 50%, median drop 0.6%") was computed on a SELECTION-BIASED set: factedit_precheck.py line 260 sorts edit_facts by -p_base then caps at N_TARGET=28, so the 28 tested facts all have baseline P>=0.71 (median 0.866) — ZERO in the [0.3,0.6) band. The pre-registration (SCREEN.md:125) only required P>0.3, and the diag shows redundancy does NOT bite at 0.3: the 3 recalled facts at P 0.34/0.36/0.38 have oracle edge-cut R = 0.21/0.52/0.41 (2 of 3 load-bearing, R>0.3), vs the hi band (P>0.6) median R=0.07, 0/12>=0.5. So the verdict "redundant on facts the model recalls" is true at P>0.6 but the NO-GO is operationally gated at P>0.3, with the entire load-bearing [0.40,0.60) zone untested by EITHER run. Worse for falsifiability: the FRA *cell-edit* (the actual GO metric) was run ONLY on the 28 dead-band facts; the diag measured only the *oracle* full edge-cut on lo/mid — so whether the SAE-feature cell-edit reaches the load-bearing edge at P 0.3-0.5 (the CCF=0.000 reach worry) is completely unresolved exactly where it could pass.

**Next test:** Re-run factedit_precheck's STAGE-3 cell-edit (FRA SAE feature-pair cell-edit, c in {1,2,4,8}) on a NEW edit set drawn from the load-bearing band P in [0.30,0.55] (relax the line-260 sort to bucket-balanced sampling, keep the same-relation partner requirement) — ~20 facts, one L4 pod, ~15 min. Report BOTH metrics: relative P(target) drop AND argmax rank-flip rate. GO-salvage iff median cell-edit drop >= 50% (or >= the oracle R at that fact) with rank-flip on a majority; if the cell-edit is still ~0 there despite oracle R~0.4, the SAE-reach (CCF=0) ceiling is the real killer and the NO-GO firms up to "FRA can't reach even the load-bearing edge."

**Bottom line:** The "redundant/G-post" mechanism is real for strongly-recalled facts (P>0.6, oracle R~0.07), so the flagship headline survives as a bound — but the NO-GO as operationalized is fragile: gate (i) was decided on a cap-28 set hand-picked to the highest-P (>=0.71) facts, excluding the entire [0.3,0.6) load-bearing band that the pre-registration (P>0.3) and the diag both implicate. The FRA cell-edit was never tested where the edge is load-bearing, and the pure-P(target) metric (no rank-flip) is biased to call sticky near-1.0 facts unmovable; a one-pod cell-edit sweep on P 0.3-0.55 is needed before the flagship negative can be called robust rather than a P>0.6-only result over-generalized to all recalled facts.
