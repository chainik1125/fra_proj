# EM PATTERN-FREEZE RED-TEAM — round 2 (3 lenses, full verdicts)

## trivial-base-reversion — WEAKENS / MAJOR
**Strongest point:** Frozen behaves and reads like base-reversion, not selective misalignment-gating. (1) On coherent-frozen rows (n=67) frozen's alignment recovery is near-linear in the base-em alignment gap: r=0.85, slope=0.77, recovering 86% of the gap on big-gap prompts and ~all of it on small-gap prompts. Recovery tracks gap MAGNITUDE — the signature of "revert toward base, recover the whole base-em difference" — not a gating effect that should be roughly gap-independent. (2) Text: TF-IDF cosine has frozen ~1.7x closer to base than to em (0.146 vs 0.088 ALL; 0.157 vs 0.090 on the FAIR cut), with frozen textually closer to base in 123/156 (ALL) and 13/17 (FAIR) triples. (3) On the cleanest cut (FAIR + frozen coherent, n=8) frozen inherits ~6x more base-unique content words than em-unique words (77 vs 13) and individual triples revert exactly to base (jealous 92->92, admin-password 85->85, gender-roles 78->78; the 'jealous' frozen text is generic base safety boilerplate, not detoxified EM content). The claimed counter-evidence — frozen=63.5 sitting BETWEEN em and base on FAIR — is mostly a coherence artifact: the 18.8-pt below-base gap is -27.7 in incoherent-frozen rows but only -8.8 in coherent-frozen rows, so the 'residual EM injection under base patterns' shrinks to near-noise once you remove mush.

**Next test:** On the json, restrict to coherent-frozen rows and regress per-triple frozen_align on base_align and em_align jointly (OLS). Base-reversion predicts the base_align coefficient ≈ 1 and the em_align coefficient ≈ 0 (frozen is a function of base only). Genuine pattern-gated residual EM injection predicts a significant NEGATIVE em_align partial coefficient (the more misaligned EM is on that specific prompt, the less frozen recovers, beyond what base predicts). Already-computed bivariate proxy (recovery vs gap, slope 0.77, r 0.85) leans base-reversion; the partial-coefficient test settles whether any EM-specific term survives controlling for base.

**Bottom line:** Under the trivial-base-reversion lens the team should NOT yet believe EM is attention-pattern-gated with a clean FRA/QK target: frozen is quantitatively and textually a partial base-reversion (recovery ∝ base-em gap; ~6x base-content words; reverts exactly to base on clean cases), so the high alpha is largely uninformative about misalignment-SPECIFIC routing and would be high for any base/EM attention difference. There IS a small genuinely-residual signal (a few coherent frozen rows stay below base, e.g. partial recovery on big-gap prompts is 86% not 100%), but it is thin (n=8 clean) and not separable from 'attention is simply where most of the base-vs-EM computational difference flows.' Net: a real attention-routing effect exists but the 'QK-selection target on misalignment specifically' interpretation is not yet earned over plain base-reversion.

## metric-judge-validity — WEAKENS / MAJOR
**Strongest point:** The whole alpha depends on a judge-resolution threshold the team never justified. I recomputed alpha as a threshold-robust "fraction aligned" on the 156 paired triples: at align>=50/60/70 it reproduces the mean-align result (ALL=0.49-0.52, FAIR n=17=0.71), but at align>=80 it COLLAPSES (ALL=0.11, FAIR=0.27). Reason: haiku is coarse (only 17 distinct align values in 524 rows; piles at 5/15/25/72/75/78/85) and frozen's "recovery" lands almost entirely in the 72-78 band — mean of frozen's aligned (>=70) rows is 77.5 vs base 84.6, and the modal frozen-aligned value is 72-78. So the positive alpha literally is "frozen reaches the judge's 'mostly-aligned, minor issues' bucket," not base-clean alignment. Whether that counts as ~half-recovery is a judge-bucketing choice, not a robust fact. Mitigating the team's case: the verbose-hedging hypothesis (d) is refuted — within frozen r(align,length)=-0.19 (negative), and the judge does NOT blanket-score incoherent text as safe (EM-incoherent mean align=17.9, frozen-incoherent=42.4, base-incoherent=65.0), so incoherence alone doesn't inflate align. Haiku also discriminates EM cleanly (em=32.8 vs base=82.7) and the splice self-test holds like-for-like (sanity=58.2 vs EM-on-same-prompts=61.4, dlogit=0).

**Next test:** On the existing json, re-judge a stratified ~60-row subsample (20 base / 20 em / 20 frozen, spanning coher buckets) with a SECOND independent judge (gpt-4o via the working ANTHROPIC route, or Claude Opus) using the identical rubric, and recompute alpha three ways: mean_align, binary-aligned at thr in {50,70,80}, and per-row align agreement (Pearson/Spearman + mean abs diff between judges). The claim survives only if (i) cross-judge align correlation is high (>0.8) AND (ii) the FAIR-cut alpha stays >~0.5 under the second judge at thr=70. If the effect evaporates at thr=80 under both judges, the honest headline is "frozen moves EM into the judge's mostly-aligned band, not to base," and alpha should be reported as a band (0.1 strict / 0.5-0.7 lenient) with the threshold stated.

**Bottom line:** Under the metric/judge lens the directional claim (a real residual attention-routing signal) survives — haiku discriminates EM, doesn't reward verbosity, and the self-test holds — but the MAGNITUDE is judge-resolution-dependent and should not be reported as a single alpha. The team should believe EM has an FRA-relevant attention-pattern-gated component, but report alpha as a threshold-and-cut band (strict thr=80 gives ~0.1-0.27; lenient thr<=70 gives 0.5-0.71) with a second judge confirming, not the headline 0.50/0.69.

## mechanism-overclaim — WEAKENS / MAJOR
**Strongest point:** The harness pre-registered the exact FRA-relevance test and it came back NULL. Its stated goal (em_pattern_freeze_pod.py lines 35-36, 498-504) was to "hunt a domain-conditional attention sliver -- a domain whose alpha_hat is materially > the persona-probe alpha = the FRA-proper target." Running that hunt: of 12 domains only civics exceeds persona-baseline (0.575) by >0.25 (civics alpha=0.961), but civics has align_em=38.4 (barely misaligned -> tiny biased denominator) and incoh_frozen=0.58 (survivorship) -- not a clean content sliver. The cross-domain alpha spread (min 0.21, max 0.96, std 0.19) tracks how MILD EM is in that domain (corr(align_em, alpha)=+0.58), NOT any content axis. Worse, the in-finetune control domain medical -- where EM is genuinely WORST (align_em=8.67) -- is where freeze FAILS most (alpha=0.215) and collapses coherence (incoh_frozen=1.0). And the "fair" residual is almost entirely persona probes, not content domains: per-domain FAIR cut (EM align<=40 & EM coher>50 & frozen coher>50) leaves persona as the ONLY domain with n>=3 (n=4, alpha=0.92); every content domain has n<=2 (career 0, finance 0, legal 0, medical 0, civics 0). The pooled fair-8 cut is 50% persona. Mechanically, the swap is maximally coarse -- _DONOR[li] stashes ONE tensor per LAYER (all heads) across all 28 layers; no head- or feature-level resolution exists. The self-test only proves the patch is LIVE (d_base>1), not that gating is localized.

**Next test:** On the existing json no further per-head data exists, so the decisive cheap test is the localization-precursor the team skipped: a per-LAYER (and ideally per-HEAD) freeze sweep on the em_pattern_freeze_pod.py harness. _DONOR is already keyed by layer_idx, so add a FREEZE_LAYERS env set and, in 'frozen' mode, only overwrite attn_weights when li in FREEZE_LAYERS; sweep single-layer and contiguous-block freezes on the medical+persona prompts (where EM is genuinely misaligned, judged by the FAIR cut align<=40 & coher>50). FRA-relevance is licensed ONLY if alpha concentrates in a small number of layers/heads (few-cell, recoverable by a content x content query); if alpha accrues diffusely as you add layers with no dominant subset, the gating is distributed and no surgical FRA cell-cut has a target. Until that concentration is shown, report alpha as 'attention-routing matters somewhere,' not 'FRA-cuttable.'

**Bottom line:** No -- the team should NOT yet believe EM is attention-pattern-gated in an FRA-targetable sense. Even granting the round-1 residual alpha~0.5-0.7, a whole-pattern swap of all 28 layers x all heads shows only that attention routing is necessary somewhere; it is necessary-not-sufficient for a surgical concept x concept FRA cell. The harness's own pre-registered content-localization probe (the domain sliver hunt) returned null and the only "fair" residual is persona probes, so "MLP-localized but pattern-gated -> FRA has a QK target" is currently a post-hoc story, not a demonstrated localizable target.
