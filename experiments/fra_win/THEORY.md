# FRA Theory (Fable, sprint-2 — refined through cycle 2)

## Mechanistic theory
WHAT FRA UNIQUELY COMPUTES. A head's pre-softmax score is a bilinear form on the residual stream: S^h[q,k] = (x_q W_Q^h)·(x_k W_K^h)/√d_h (row-vector convention; biases give pure row/column/constant terms). Substituting the SAE decomposition x_t = Σ_μ u^μ_t f_μ + e_t yields the EXACT partition S^h[q,k] = Σ_{μν} u^μ_q u^ν_k ω^h_{μν} + (feature×error) + (error×error) + bias terms, with ω^h_{μν} = (f_μ W_Q^h)·(f_ν W_K^h)/√d_h the weight-space pair coupling. Each (μ,ν) CELL is a multiplicative AND: its value is the product u^μ_q·u^ν_k, identically zero unless the query-content feature AND the key-content feature both fire (u ≥ 0 under ReLU/JumpReLU). This is the only exact additive decomposition in the attention pathway that lives in the JOINT (query-content × key-content) function space: the OV side (C^OV = A·u^λ·⟨f_λ W_OV, d⟩) is merely linear-given-pattern, and the post-softmax pattern admits no canonical decomposition (the denominator couples all cells across all keys — attribution needs the Jacobian ∂A_qk/∂s_qj = A_qk(1_{k=j}−A_qj), giving δT ≈ −u^μ_q u^ν_k ω_{μν}·g̃^h_{q,k} with the centered OV profile g̃^h_{q,j}=A^h_{qj}(g^h_j−ḡ^h_q); but the EDIT needs no linearization because it acts on s before the softmax). THE EDIT AND ITS EXPRESSIVE UNIQUENESS. The FRA edit subtracts c·u^μ_q u^ν_k ω^h_{μν} from ONE head's scores wherever the conjunction fires. With the SAE gates frozen it is exactly a support-gated rank-1 update of the head's bilinear form, ΔW^h_QK ∝ −c·ω_{μν}·e_μᵀe_ν restricted to {(q,k): u^μ_q>0 ∧ u^ν_k>0}; its only causal channel is that head's softmax → OV transport. A linear residual steer Δ (ActAdd/DoM/CAA/projection-removal), even gated by an ARBITRARY content gate g(x), is doubly weaker. (a) SCORE-SPACE EXPRESSIVITY: a gated query-side write changes scores by δs[q,k] = g(x_q)·(Δ W_Q^h)·(x_k W_K^h)ᵀ/√d, which varies over keys only through x_k's projection onto ONE fixed pullback direction v = Δ W_Q^h W_K^hᵀ. Cancelling a single cell requires ⟨x_k, v⟩ ∝ u^ν(x_k) for ALL k — impossible: u^ν is a thresholded nonlinearity of x_k, and even its linear part forces hitting the entire ν-ROW of the bilinear form plus every key cosine-close to v. A steer can imitate a row or a column of W_QK, never a cell; and (cycle-1 extension) FRA additionally supports SET ALGEBRA over cells — unions across heads/pairs and DIFFERENCES (differential cells = target-edge top-M minus sibling-edge top-M) — which no rank-constrained linear object in residual space can express at all. (b) RESIDUAL BROADCAST: the write Δ is deposited into the stream and read by EVERY consumer at the gated positions — all heads' Q, K AND V, the MLP, every later layer. Gating restricts WHERE the steer fires, never WHAT reads it. These two facts are the measured separability gap: at matched on-target removal a content-gated projection-removal steer strong enough to suppress the behavior destroys the content everywhere it legitimately appears (legit-content KL 1.77–2.64 nats), while the FRA cell-edit leaves it at ~0.000–0.0016 (≈1,000–26,000×), because legitimate occurrences fail the conjunction. THE MAGNITUDE LAW (validated in cycle 1): A ≈ reuse(marginal endpoint)/reuse(conjunction), with reuse measured ON THE EVAL/DEPLOYMENT SUPPORT, not naive corpus marginals (the s5 corpus-firing-rate operationalization is degenerate). Both extremes are now measured: A ~10³–10⁴× when the conjunction is unique (retrieval, acronym), and A→1.9× floor when a GENERIC endpoint makes the conjunction recur (shared-endpoint sibling test: a generic 'box-query' feature means (query × frog-value) recurs for the sibling, so FRA halves the sibling too). Corollary: selectivity is purchasable by differential cells (A 1.9→7.9× with sibling preserved) at a reach cost (on-target removal 58%→33%), because selectivity spends the score mass living in shared cells. NONLINEARITY LEDGER (what is exact where): RMSNorm is a frozen per-position SCALAR ×diag(γ) — it commutes into the activations u given the cached rms, so the score decomposition is exact on Gemma (edge-magnitude ratio 1.07 confirms). GPT-2 LayerNorm adds the fixed centering projector (I−11ᵀ/d); the ~50–75% edge-reconstruction shortfall is a LN-folding-convention artifact, not an FRA property; gold-standard hookpoint is ln1.hook_normalized but no public SAE suite ships it, and the grms check showed the hookpoint is not the limiter on RMSNorm models. RoPE is a per-position ROTATION applied AFTER projection: rotate each per-feature projected q/k vector, making the coupling relative-offset-indexed, ω^h_{μν}(q−k) = ((f_μ W_Q^h)R_q)·((f_ν W_K^h)R_k)ᵀ/√d (depends only on q−k by the block structure R_qR_kᵀ=R_{q−k}) — still exact per edge; the edit stays content-addressed with an offset-dependent coefficient. GPT-2's learned absolute positional embeddings enter simply as residual content (positional features → the CCF sink-mask handles them). Gemma's soft-cap s ↦ 50·tanh(s/50) is the ONE true nonlinearity between the bilinear sum and the softmax: cells are exact pre-cap, and an edit Δs reaches the pattern attenuated by tanh′(s/50) ≤ 1, so edits are compressed exactly on saturated edges. This — plus the SAE-unexplained score fraction (the error×· terms) and granularity×top-K fragmentation (1M-width SAEs split an edge across too many tiny cells for a fixed pair budget; 65k is the GemmaScope sweet spot) — is the complete account of FRA's suppression-reach ceiling. The ceiling bounds REACH, never separability: whatever FRA removes, it removes only on the conjunction's support. SUMMARY: FRA computes the exact joint query-content×key-content functional decomposition of attention selection, and hands you the corresponding cell-level (and cell-set-level) weight-edit whose causal support is a content conjunction — the missing middle between direction steers (marginal + broadcast) and position patches (specific but not content-addressed).

## Win checklist
1. EDGE-ROUTED (CCF-high): the behavior's proximate cause is pre-softmax score mass on identifiable attention edges, and that mass is dominated by non-sink content-feature × content-feature pairs after sink-masking (CCF reliable on gpt2; on gemma the current sink-mask over-kills late layers, so certify this clause behaviorally via LBNR). Kills positional/BOS edges (CCF=0.000) and output-direction behaviors with no score-space cause (weight-baked sleeper).
2. LOAD-BEARING & NON-REDUNDANT (LBNR-R ≈ 1, measured BEHAVIORALLY with CAUSAL head-finding): cutting the identified feature-pair edge across ALL heads that carry it (heads ranked by causal edge-cut, never by raw attention — raw attention picks positional heads) removes the downstream behavior, and no backup circuit restores it. Kills IOI (R=−0.39, backups overcompensate), docstring (R=0.08), gemma knowledge-conflict (R=0.24).
3. DIRECT CONSUMPTION: the behavior IS the edge's OV-transported content (the attended token's write into the output), not a downstream MLP computation that merely reads the attended info — so LBNR must be evaluated on the behavioral metric, not on attention or an intermediate. Kills greater-than (the > is MLP-computed; edge-cut dents behavior only 0.98→0.87).
4. CONJUNCTION-SPECIFIC ON THE EVAL DISTRIBUTION (sharpened by cycle 1): at least one endpoint feature legitimately recurs in deployment (so every direction/content-gated steer pays collateral), while the specific conjunction does NOT recur legitimately — which requires the discriminating endpoint (usually the QUERY) to be TARGET-SPECIFIC, not a generic role feature; reuse must be measured on the eval-distribution support, not naive corpus firing rates (the s5 failure). If siblings share the value endpoint, differential cells (target-edge minus sibling-edge) must retain enough non-shared score mass to act on. Kills rare-dedicated-token triggers (a direction is already surgical, A→1) and generic-role-query setups (sibling A=1.9×).
5. REACH (the only item bounding removal completeness rather than the win/lose verdict): the SAE-explained fraction of the edge score × head coverage × top-K pair budget — with granularity matched to K (65k GemmaScope sweet spot; 1M fragments at fixed K) — × soft-cap headroom tanh′(s/50) must clear the softmax margin on every carrying head at faithful scale c≈1–2, so a matched-removal operating point exists at which the separability comparison is defined.

**Underlying reason:** The whole checklist is one separability statement. The behavior's minimal sufficient cause must be a SEPARABLE PRODUCT TERM u^μ_q·u^ν_k inside one (or an enumerable few) heads' score functions, whose support is disjoint from legitimate use of its endpoints ON THE EVAL DISTRIBUTION. When it is: (i) the FRA edit's support is exactly the conjunction's support and its only causal channel is those heads' softmaxes, so its collateral scales with reuse(conjunction|eval) — ~0 when the pairing is the targeted/malicious thing; (ii) ANY linear residual steer, however gated, must either suppress a marginal endpoint — its score effect is confined to a row/column of the bilinear form (one fixed pullback direction over keys) — or write into the residual stream where all heads' Q/K/V, the MLP, and every later layer read it; so its collateral scales with reuse(marginal|eval) × (number of residual readers). The win ratio at matched removal is therefore A ≈ reuse(marginal|eval)/reuse(conjunction|eval) — now VALIDATED at both extremes: ~10³–10⁴× when the conjunction is unique (retrieval 1100×, acronym 26000×) and a ~1.9× floor when a generic endpoint makes the conjunction recur (shared-endpoint sibling test), with differential cells buying specificity back (7.9×) at a quantified reach cost (58%→33%). CCF certifies the cause lives in score space; LBNR certifies the conjunction is the unique carrier; direct-consumption certifies the product term's output IS the behavior; conjunction-specificity certifies the ratio's denominator is small and numerator large; reach certifies the basis can express enough of the term to act on. Each failure mode is the negation of exactly one factor.

**Failure modes:**
- OUTPUT-DIRECTION ROUTING (weight-baked sleeper, emergent misalignment): the payload is written through OV/unembedding as a residual DIRECTION with near-zero legitimate reuse; there is no score-space cause to edit, and DoM/SVD on the marginal direction is already surgical — no bilinear gap exists (negates edge-routing + the reuse gap).
- REDUNDANT / SELF-REPAIRING CIRCUITS (IOI name-movers R=−0.39 with backup attention 0.34→0.69; docstring R=0.08): cutting the edge triggers backup heads that restore — or overshoot — the behavior; the conjunction is not the unique carrier, so the edit is precise but behaviorally inert (negates LBNR).
- DISTRIBUTED / DIRECTION-ROUTED IN-CONTEXT BEHAVIOR (many-shot jailbreak; binding R=−0.89; gemma knowledge-conflict R=0.24): the behavior rides a distributed ICL task-direction across many weak edges, not a separable pair; worse, the injected behavior can be representationally identical to a legitimate one ('jailbroken enthusiasm' = 'genuine enthusiasm'), so NO method removes it cleanly — there is no separable link to cut (negates edge-routing + LBNR).
- DOWNSTREAM NONLINEAR CONSUMPTION (greater-than): the QK edge is real and content-conjunctive, but an MLP computes the behavior from the attended information; cutting the edge controls the attending, not the computation (negates direct consumption).
- CONJUNCTION RECURRENCE — collision or generic endpoint (acronym 'Unit' duplicate; shared-endpoint sibling A=1.9×): the target conjunction's support overlaps legitimate instances of the SAME pair, either literally or because an endpoint is a generic role feature ('box-query'), so FRA's content-addressing correctly fires on them too — collateral by design, A collapses toward reuse(marginal)/reuse(conjunction) ≈ 1. Partial rescue: differential cells restore target-specificity (1.9→7.9×) at reach cost (negates conjunction-specificity).
- REACH CEILING (Gemma removal 1/4→3/4 backdoors at 16k→65k; 1M-width regression at fixed K; GPT-2 LN-centering 50–75% reconstruction; soft-cap compression tanh′(s/50) on saturated edges): the SAE basis is too coarse, too fine for the pair budget, folding drops centering, or the cap eats the edit — FRA cannot hit the matched-removal operating point, so the win is capped or undefined even though separability holds for what it can remove (negates reach).
- POSITIONAL / SINK EDGE (BOS: raw attention 0.98 but CCF=0.000; lost-in-the-middle): the edge is selected by position, not content; a position-patch is the correct tool and content-addressing adds nothing (negates the content clause of edge-routing).

## Synthetic settings
- **Induction cue-suppression (gpt2-small) — expressivity anchor** (known): One known bilinear gate — (query=X) × (key=token-after-previous-X) — with no semantics, no MLP involvement, and enumerable head redundancy (hit all 5 heads); support-not-rigged and faithful-scale (c≈2) checks already passed. — Pure-conjunction expressivity: a rank-1 cell edit suppresses an association with near-zero off-conjunction support (15× vs the strongest gated linear steer; 235× locality).
- **In-context trigger→payload backdoor (gpt2 + gemma) — reuse-flip anchor** (known): Trigger and payload are NORMAL tokens planted in the prompt, so marginal-reuse is high by construction while conjunction-reuse is zero; identical task to the weight-baked sleeper FRA lost, so the ranking flip (FRA 0.07 nats vs DoM 1.83 / conv-SAE 6.06) isolates WHERE the association lives as the only variable. — The marginal≫conjunction reuse bound, against the exact baselines that win the direction-routed case.
- **Two-needle key-value retrieval (gemma-2-2b) — corrected-headline separability anchor** (known): RMSNorm makes the FRA correction exact (edge ratio 1.07), removing the reconstruction confound; random off-edge null is clean; the ~1100× legit-content-KL win vs the content-gated projection-removal steer is the program's decisive fair-baseline result. — The decisive separability claim itself (legit-content KL at matched removal vs the strongest fair baseline) under exact normalization.
- **Shared-endpoint sibling test + differential-cell rescue (gemma, shared_endpoint_t2) — the conjunction-recurrence floor** (known): Two boxes hold the same value; the generic 'box-query' feature makes the conjunction recur for the sibling — the ONLY moving variable vs the 2-needle win is endpoint genericity. Measured: A=1.9× full-pair, 7.9× with differential cells at 58%→33% reach. — The magnitude law A ≈ reuse(marginal)/reuse(conjunction) at its degenerate floor, and the selectivity-costs-reach trade-off of differential cells.
- **Query-specificity dial** (NEW): Parametrically morph the query endpoint from target-specific to generic role: K key-value boxes whose retrieval cue ranges from unique-per-box ('the red box', distinct lexical cues) to fully shared ('the box', position-only disambiguation), everything else fixed — conjunction recurrence n/K is the single knob, measured directly as FRA-mass overlap between target and sibling edges. — Turns the validated two-point magnitude law into a CURVE: A(n/K) should fall from the retrieval-win regime (~10³) to the sibling floor (~2) as the discriminating query feature delocalizes; also locates the crossover where differential cells stop helping (no non-shared mass left). This is the cycle-2 lesson (query content must be the discriminator) as a quantitative prediction.
- **Differential-cell reach–selectivity frontier** (NEW): Fixed sibling setup; sweep the differential-cell budget M and scale c, mapping the (on-target removal, sibling collateral) Pareto. Both axes are exact behavioral quantities; the cell decomposition gives a parameter-free prediction for each point (removal ∝ score mass of selected cells; sibling collateral ∝ mass of selected cells that also live on the sibling edge). — The corollary that selectivity is set-algebra over cells purchasable at reach cost — and whether the collateral floor equals the cell-support overlap, which would make 'how selective can FRA be made' computable in advance from one forward pass.
- **Redundancy dial (synthetic backup circuit)** (NEW): Carry one planted association over R parallel routes (duplicate demonstrations at different offsets so distinct induction edges/layers each suffice); LBNR-R degrades continuously and controllably, unlike natural backups (IOI) which are all-or-nothing. — LBNR as a threshold: at what redundancy does edge-cut R fall below the win boundary — and does cutting the UNION of conjunctions (FRA can address all R cells at once; a position-patch cannot) restore the win? Distinguishes 'the EDGE is unique' from 'the CONJUNCTION is unique'.
- **MLP-interposition probe** (NEW): Plant an in-context association whose payload is not the attended token but a fixed downstream FUNCTION of it (attend to a digit, emit its successor via known successor circuitry): the QK edge is identical to the winning backdoor case; only consumption changes. — The direct-consumption bound as a controlled dissociation: FRA should cut attendance fully while behavioral removal degrades exactly with how much the MLP re-derives the answer from residual leftovers — the greater-than failure mode, isolated.
- **Soft-cap saturation stress (gemma) with uncapped gpt2 contrast arm** (NEW): Same planted edge at controlled baseline score margins (push pre-cap scores into and out of the 50·tanh(s/50) regime by stacking demonstrations); tanh′(s/50) gives a parameter-free attenuation prediction; gpt2 (no cap) is the control. — The soft-cap clause of the reach bound: edit-effectiveness on the pattern should track tanh′(s/50), confirming the cap (not the SAE) as Gemma's marginal reach limiter on saturated edges — and that separability is unchanged at every attainable operating point.
- **Full MQAR row×column grid (multi-query associative recall, Arora et al. 2023 style)** (NEW): Key→value context where the SAME value appears under two keys AND the same key-type queries multiple values: deleting one association forces a steer to damage an entire ROW AND COLUMN of in-use associations within a single prompt — the maximal-stress separability setting, on an established ICL-literature benchmark, requiring differential cells in BOTH directions. — The underlying separability reason in its purest form (cell vs row/column expressivity), and whether two-sided differential-cell selection composes — the strongest synthetic demonstration available that the bilinear structure, not content-addressing, is load-bearing.
