# FRA Theory (Fable, sprint-2 — converged @ cycle 3)

## Mechanistic theory
WHAT FRA UNIQUELY COMPUTES. A head h's pre-softmax score is a bilinear form on the post-norm residual: S^h[q,k] = (x_q W_Q^h)·(x_k W_K^h)/√d_h (row-vector convention; biases contribute pure row/column/constant terms). Substituting the SAE decomposition x_t = Σ_μ u^μ_t f_μ + e_t yields the EXACT partition S^h[q,k] = Σ_{μν} u^μ_q·u^ν_k·ω^h_{μν} + (feature×error) + (error×error) + bias terms, with ω^h_{μν} = (f_μ W_Q^h)·(f_ν W_K^h)/√d_h the weight-space pair coupling. Each (μ,ν) CELL is a multiplicative AND: its value u^μ_q·u^ν_k is identically zero unless the query-content feature AND the key-content feature both fire (u ≥ 0 under ReLU/JumpReLU). This is the only exact additive decomposition in the attention pathway that lives in the JOINT (query-content × key-content) function space: the OV side (C^OV = A·u^λ·⟨f_λ W_OV, d⟩) is merely linear-given-pattern, and the post-softmax pattern admits no canonical decomposition (the denominator couples all cells across all keys; attribution requires the Jacobian ∂A_qk/∂s_qj = A_qk(1_{k=j}−A_qj) and the centered OV profile g̃^h_{q,j} = A^h_{qj}(g^h_j−ḡ^h_q) — but the EDIT needs no linearization because it acts on s BEFORE the softmax). THE EDIT AND ITS EXPRESSIVE UNIQUENESS. The FRA edit subtracts c·u^μ_q·u^ν_k·ω^h_{μν} from one head's scores wherever the conjunction fires. With the SAE gates frozen it is exactly a support-gated rank-1 update of the head's bilinear form, ΔW^h_QK ∝ −c·ω_{μν}·e_μᵀe_ν restricted to {(q,k): u^μ_q>0 ∧ u^ν_k>0}; its only causal channel is that head's softmax→OV transport. FRA additionally supports SET ALGEBRA over cells: UNIONS across pairs/heads — including whole feature FAMILIES (class-level edits: cut every (attribute-query × value-class-key) cell at once) — and DIFFERENCES (differential cells = target-edge top-M minus sibling-edge top-M), which buy back target-specificity when siblings share an endpoint (measured: sibling separability 1.9×→7.9× at a quantified reach cost 58%→33%). A linear residual steer Δ (ActAdd/DoM/CAA/projection-removal), even gated by an ARBITRARY content gate g(x), is doubly weaker. (a) SCORE-SPACE EXPRESSIVITY: a gated query-side write changes scores by δs[q,k] = g(x_q)·(Δ W_Q^h)·(x_k W_K^h)ᵀ/√d, which varies over keys only through x_k's projection onto ONE fixed pullback direction v = Δ W_Q^h W_K^hᵀ. Cancelling a single cell requires ⟨x_k, v⟩ ∝ u^ν(x_k) for ALL k — impossible: u^ν is a thresholded nonlinearity of x_k, and even its linear part forces hitting the entire ν-ROW of the bilinear form plus every key cosine-close to v. A steer can imitate a row or a column of W_QK, never a cell — and a fortiori never a cell-difference or a family-union. (b) RESIDUAL BROADCAST: the write Δ is deposited into the stream and read by EVERY consumer at the gated positions — all heads' Q, K AND V, the MLP, every later layer. Gating restricts WHERE the steer fires, never WHAT reads it. These two facts ARE the measured separability gap: at matched on-target removal, a content-gated projection-removal steer strong enough to suppress the behavior destroys the content everywhere it legitimately appears (legit-content KL 1.77–2.64 nats), while the FRA cell-edit leaves it at ~0.000–0.0016 (≈1,000–26,000×), because legitimate occurrences fail the conjunction. THE MAGNITUDE LAW (validated at both extremes): A ≈ reuse(marginal endpoint)/reuse(conjunction), with reuse measured ON THE EVAL/DEPLOYMENT SUPPORT (naive corpus firing rates are degenerate — the s5 failure). A ~ 10³–10⁴ when the conjunction is unique (retrieval 1100×, acronym 26,000×); A → ~1.9× floor when a GENERIC endpoint makes the conjunction recur (shared-endpoint sibling test). SHARPENED COROLLARY (cycle 2): the discriminating endpoint — usually the query — must be distinctive CONTENT (a specific token/feature), NOT a generic ROLE feature. Positionally-resolved retrieval (box/entity identity) uses a generic "queried-slot" feature, so the (query × value) conjunction recurs across siblings and FRA cannot separate them (box A=7.9× partial only at weak base; entity-PII no-op at strong base). Every confirmed win has a content query: induction (query = token X), copy-suppression (query = about-to-predict-X), acronym (query = the spelled letters), in-context backdoor (query = the trigger content). NONLINEARITY LEDGER (what is exact where): RMSNorm is a frozen per-position SCALAR × diag(γ) — it commutes into the activations given the cached rms, so the score decomposition is exact on Gemma (edge-magnitude ratio 1.07 confirms). GPT-2 LayerNorm adds the fixed centering projector (I−11ᵀ/d); the 50–75% edge-reconstruction shortfall is a LN-folding-convention artifact, not an FRA property; ln1.hook_normalized is the gold-standard hookpoint but no public SAE suite ships it, and the grms check showed the hookpoint is not the limiter on RMSNorm models. RoPE is a per-position ROTATION applied AFTER projection: ω^h_{μν}(q−k) = ((f_μ W_Q^h)R_q)·((f_ν W_K^h)R_k)ᵀ/√d depends only on the relative offset (block structure R_qR_kᵀ = R_{q−k}) — still exact per edge; the edit stays content-addressed with an offset-dependent coefficient. GPT-2's learned absolute positional embeddings enter simply as residual content (positional features → the CCF sink-mask handles them). Gemma's soft-cap s ↦ 50·tanh(s/50) is the ONE true nonlinearity between the bilinear sum and the softmax: cells are exact pre-cap, and an edit Δs reaches the pattern attenuated by tanh′(s/50) ≤ 1, so edits are compressed exactly on saturated edges. This — plus the SAE-unexplained score fraction (the error×· terms) and granularity×top-K matching (65k GemmaScope is the sweet spot; 1M-width fragments an edge across too many tiny cells for a fixed pair budget) — is the complete account of FRA's suppression-reach ceiling. The ceiling bounds REACH, never separability: whatever FRA removes, it removes only on the conjunction's support. SUMMARY: FRA computes the exact joint query-content×key-content functional decomposition of attention selection, and hands you the corresponding cell-level (and cell-set-level) weight edit whose causal support is a content conjunction — the missing middle between direction steers (marginal + broadcast to all readers) and position patches (specific but not content-addressed).

## Win checklist
1. EDGE-ROUTED (CCF-high): the behavior's proximate cause is pre-softmax score mass on identifiable attention edges, dominated by non-sink content×content feature pairs after sink-masking (CCF reliable on gpt2-small; on gemma the current sink-mask over-kills late layers, so certify behaviorally via LBNR). Kills positional/BOS edges (CCF=0.000) and output-direction behaviors with no score-space cause (weight-baked sleeper).
2. LOAD-BEARING & NON-REDUNDANT (LBNR-R ≈ 1, measured BEHAVIORALLY with CAUSAL head-finding): cutting the identified feature-pair edge across ALL heads that carry it (heads ranked by causal edge-cut effect, never raw attention — raw attention selects positional/sink heads) removes the downstream behavior, and no backup circuit OR alternative cue restores it. Kills IOI (R=−0.39, backups overcompensate), docstring (R=0.08), delimiter matching (R=0.23, grammar provides redundant cues), gemma knowledge-conflict (R=0.24).
3. DIRECT CONSUMPTION: the behavior IS the edge's OV-transported content (the attended token's write into the output), not a downstream MLP computation that merely reads the attended information — so LBNR must be evaluated on the behavioral metric, never on attention or an intermediate. Kills greater-than (the > is MLP-computed; edge-cut dents behavior only 0.98→0.87).
4. CONJUNCTION-SPECIFIC ON THE EVAL DISTRIBUTION, with a DISTINCTIVE-CONTENT discriminating endpoint: at least one endpoint feature legitimately recurs in deployment (so every direction/content-gated steer pays collateral), while the specific conjunction does NOT recur legitimately — which requires the discriminating endpoint (usually the query) to be distinctive CONTENT (a specific token/phrase/letter-state feature), not a generic ROLE feature; positionally-resolved identity (box/entity slots) fails this clause by construction. If siblings share the value endpoint, differential cells (target-edge minus sibling-edge) must retain enough non-shared score mass; if the goal is class-level removal, the UNION of the family's cells must be enumerable. Kills rare-dedicated-token triggers (a direction is already surgical, A→1) and generic-role-query retrieval (sibling A=1.9×; entity-PII no-op).
5. REACH (bounds removal completeness, not the win/lose verdict): SAE-explained fraction of the edge score × causal head coverage × top-K pair budget — with SAE granularity matched to K (65k GemmaScope sweet spot; 1M fragments at fixed K) — × soft-cap headroom tanh′(s/50) must clear the softmax margin on every carrying head at faithful scale c≈1–2, so that a matched-removal operating point EXISTS at which the separability comparison is defined.

**Underlying reason:** The whole checklist is one separability statement. The behavior's minimal sufficient cause must be a SEPARABLE PRODUCT TERM u^μ_q·u^ν_k inside one (or an enumerable few) heads' score functions, whose support is disjoint from legitimate use of its endpoints ON THE EVAL DISTRIBUTION. When it is: (i) the FRA edit's support is exactly the conjunction's support and its only causal channel is those heads' softmaxes, so its collateral scales with reuse(conjunction|eval) — ~0 when the pairing is the targeted/malicious thing; (ii) ANY linear residual steer, however gated, must either suppress a marginal endpoint — its score effect is confined to a row/column of the bilinear form (one fixed pullback direction over keys) — or write into the residual stream where all heads' Q/K/V, the MLP, and every later layer read it; so its collateral scales with reuse(marginal|eval) × (number of residual readers). The win ratio at matched removal is therefore A ≈ reuse(marginal|eval)/reuse(conjunction|eval), validated at both extremes: ~10³–10⁴ when the conjunction is unique (retrieval 1100×, acronym 26,000×) and a ~1.9× floor when a generic role endpoint makes the conjunction recur (shared-endpoint sibling), with differential cells buying specificity back (7.9×) at a quantified reach cost (58%→33%). CCF certifies the cause lives in score space; LBNR certifies the conjunction is the unique carrier (against both backup circuits and redundant distributional cues); direct-consumption certifies the product term's output IS the behavior; conjunction-specificity-with-content-discriminator certifies the ratio's denominator is small and its numerator large; reach certifies the basis can express enough of the term to act on. Each failure mode is the negation of exactly one factor.

**Failure modes:**
- OUTPUT-DIRECTION ROUTING (weight-baked sleeper, emergent misalignment): the payload is written through OV/unembedding as a residual DIRECTION with near-zero legitimate reuse; there is no score-space cause to edit, and DoM/SVD on the marginal direction is already surgical — no bilinear gap exists (negates edge-routing and the reuse gap).
- REDUNDANT / SELF-REPAIRING CIRCUITS (IOI name-movers R=−0.39 with backup attention 0.34→0.69; docstring R=0.08): cutting the edge triggers backup heads that restore — or overshoot — the behavior; the conjunction is not the unique carrier, so the edit is precise but behaviorally inert (negates LBNR).
- DISTRIBUTIONAL REDUNDANCY OF STRUCTURAL PREDICTION (delimiter/quote-type matching, CCF=0.732 but R=+0.23 — the cycle-2 negative class): the edge is a genuine content×content conjunction, but the predicted output is ALSO derivable from many other cues the distribution provides (grammar gives many ways to know a paren must close), so the edge is not load-bearing even with no dedicated backup circuit (negates LBNR by cue-redundancy rather than head-redundancy).
- DISTRIBUTED / DIRECTION-ROUTED IN-CONTEXT BEHAVIOR (many-shot jailbreak; binding R=−0.89; gemma knowledge-conflict R=0.24): the behavior rides a distributed ICL task-direction across many weak edges, not a separable pair; worse, the injected behavior can be representationally identical to a legitimate one ('jailbroken enthusiasm' = 'genuine enthusiasm'), so NO method removes it cleanly — there is no separable link to cut (negates edge-routing and LBNR).
- DOWNSTREAM NONLINEAR CONSUMPTION (greater-than): the QK edge is real and content-conjunctive, but an MLP computes the behavior from the attended information; cutting the edge controls the attending, not the computation (negates direct consumption).
- CONJUNCTION RECURRENCE — generic role endpoint, positional identity, or collision (shared-endpoint sibling A=1.9×; entity-PII differential no-op at strong base; acronym 'Unit' duplicate): the target conjunction's support overlaps legitimate instances of the SAME pair, either literally (duplicate target word) or because the discriminating endpoint is a generic role feature ('box-query', 'entity-slot') so identity is resolved POSITIONALLY, not by content — FRA's content-addressing correctly fires on the siblings too; collateral by design, A collapses toward 1. Partial rescue: differential cells (1.9→7.9×) at reach cost, IF non-shared mass exists (negates conjunction-specificity).
- REACH CEILING (Gemma backdoor removal 1/4→3/4 at 16k→65k; 1M-width regression at fixed K; GPT-2 LN-centering 50–75% reconstruction; soft-cap compression tanh′(s/50) on saturated edges; uncovered carrying heads): the SAE basis is too coarse, too fine for the pair budget, the folding drops centering, the cap eats the edit, or a carrying head is missed — FRA cannot hit the matched-removal operating point, so the win is capped or undefined even though separability holds for what it can remove (negates reach).
- POSITIONAL / SINK EDGE (BOS: raw attention 0.98 but CCF=0.000; lost-in-the-middle): the edge is selected by position, not content; a position-patch is the correct tool and content-addressing adds nothing (negates the content clause of edge-routing).
