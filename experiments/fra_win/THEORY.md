# FRA Theory (Fable theory agent, sprint-2 cycle 1)

## Mechanistic theory
WHAT FRA UNIQUELY COMPUTES. An attention head's pre-softmax score is a bilinear form on the residual stream: S[q,k] = x_q W_QK x_k^T/√d_h with W_QK = W_Q W_K^T. FRA re-expresses this quadratic form in SAE-decoder coordinates: substituting x_t = Σ_μ u^μ_t f_μ + e_t gives the EXACT partition S[q,k] = Σ_{μν} u^μ_q u^ν_k ω^h_{μν} + (feature×error) + (error×error) + bias terms, where ω^h_{μν} = (f_μ W_Q)·(f_ν W_K)/√d_h is the weight-space pair coupling. Each (μ,ν) cell is a MULTIPLICATIVE AND: its value is the product u^μ_q·u^ν_k, identically zero unless the query-content feature AND the key-content feature both fire. This is the only exact additive decomposition in the attention pathway that lives in the JOINT (query-content × key-content) function space; the OV side (C^OV = A·u^λ·⟨f_λ W_OV, d⟩) is merely linear-given-pattern, and the post-softmax pattern admits no canonical decomposition (the softmax denominator couples all cells — hence attribution uses the Jacobian ∂A_qk/∂s_qj = A_qk(1_{k=j} − A_qj), giving δT ≈ −u^μ_q u^ν_k ω_{μν} · g̃^h_{q,k} with the centered OV profile g̃ — but the EDIT needs no linearization because it acts on s before the softmax). WHY THE RANK-1 W_QK EDIT IS EXPRESSIVELY UNIQUE. The FRA edit subtracts c·u^μ_q u^ν_k ω_{μν} from one head's scores wherever the conjunction fires — equivalently a rank-1 update of the head's bilinear form restricted to the (f_μ, f_ν) feature cell (an exact weight edit when the SAE gates are frozen; implemented as the content-addressed score subtraction). Its support is exactly {(q,k): u^μ_q>0 ∧ u^ν_k>0} and its only causal channel is one head's softmax. A linear residual steer Δ (ActAdd/DoM/CAA/projection-removal), even content-gated by an arbitrary gate g(x), is doubly weaker: (a) EXPRESSIVITY IN SCORE SPACE — a query-side write changes scores by δs[q,k] = g(x_q)·(Δ W_Q)·(x_k W_K)^T/√d, which varies over keys only through x_k's projection onto ONE fixed direction W_K^T W_Q^T Δ^T. To cancel a single (μ,ν) cell it would need (Δ W_QK x_k^T) ∝ u^ν(x_k) for all k — impossible: the left side is linear in x_k, u^ν is a ReLU/JumpReLU nonlinearity, and even the linear part forces hitting the entire ν-ROW of the bilinear form plus every key whose content is cosine-close to that pullback direction. A steer can imitate a row or column of W_QK, never a cell. (b) SIDE-EFFECTS IN RESIDUAL SPACE — the write Δ is deposited into the residual stream and read by EVERY consumer at the gated positions: all heads' Q, K AND V projections at that layer, the MLP, and all downstream layers. Gating restricts WHERE the steer fires, not WHAT reads it. This is the measured separability gap: at matched on-target removal, a projection-removal steer strong enough to suppress the behavior destroys the content everywhere it legitimately appears (legit-content KL 1.77–2.64 nats), while the FRA cell-edit leaves it at ~0.000–0.0016 (≈1,000–26,000×), because legitimate occurrences fail the conjunction. NONLINEARITY ACCOUNTING (what is exact where): RMSNorm is a frozen per-position SCALAR (×diag γ) that commutes into the activations u — the score decomposition is exact given the cached rms (Gemma edge-magnitude ratio 1.07, confirming exactness); GPT-2 LayerNorm adds the centering projector (I − 11^T/d), a fixed linear map that our folding convention partially drops → the ~50–75% edge-reconstruction caveat is an implementation artifact of LN-folding, not of FRA. RoPE is a per-position ROTATION applied after projection: apply R_pos to each per-feature projected q/k vector before the dot product; the pair coupling becomes relative-offset-indexed, ω^h_{μν}(k−q) = (f_μ W_Q R_q)·(f_ν W_K R_k)^T/√d — still exact per edge, and the edit stays content-addressed with a position-dependent coefficient. Gemma's attention soft-cap s ↦ 50·tanh(s/50) is the ONE true nonlinearity between the bilinear sum and the softmax: cells are exact pre-cap; an edit Δs reaches the pattern attenuated by tanh'(s/50) ≤ 1, so edits are compressed exactly on saturated edges — this, plus the SAE-unexplained score fraction (the error×· terms) and granularity×top-K fragmentation (1M-width SAEs split an edge across too many tiny pairs for fixed K), is the complete account of FRA's suppression-reach ceiling. The ceiling bounds REACH, never the separability ratio: FRA removes only the FRA-explained part of an edge, but what it removes, it removes only on the conjunction's support.

## Bounds — win checklist
1. EDGE-ROUTED (CCF-high): the behavior's proximate cause is pre-softmax score mass on specific attention edges, and that mass is dominated by non-sink content-feature × content-feature pairs (CCF ≫ 0 after masking ubiquitous sink features). Kills: positional/BOS edges (CCF=0.000), output-direction behaviors like the weight-baked sleeper (no score-space cause at all).
2. LOAD-BEARING & NON-REDUNDANT (LBNR-R ≈ 1, measured BEHAVIORALLY): cutting the identified feature-pair edge across ALL heads that carry it (heads ranked by causal edge-cut, never by raw attention) removes the downstream behavior, and no backup circuit restores it. Kills: IOI (R=−0.39, backups overcompensate), docstring (R=0.08, redundant), many-shot jailbreak (distributed).
3. DIRECT CONSUMPTION: the behavior must BE the edge's OV-transported content (the attended token's write into the output), not a downstream MLP computation that merely reads the attended info — i.e., LBNR must be evaluated on the behavioral metric, not on attention or an intermediate. Kills: greater-than (the > is MLP-computed; edge-cut dents behavior only 0.98→0.87).
4. MARGINAL-REUSE ≫ CONJUNCTION-REUSE: at least one endpoint feature (the query-content or key-content) legitimately recurs in the deployment distribution, while the specific conjunction does NOT recur legitimately. This is what makes every direction/content-gated steer pay collateral and what makes FRA's collateral ~0; if the trigger is a rare dedicated token (sleeper) a direction is already surgical and the FRA advantage → 1×; if the conjunction itself recurs legitimately (acronym 'Unit' duplicate) FRA correctly fires there too and the advantage collapses by design.
5. REACH: the SAE-explained fraction of the edge score × head coverage × top-K pair budget must clear the softmax margin on every carrying head at faithful scale (c≈1–2), with granularity matched to K (65k sweet spot on GemmaScope; 1M fragments) and headroom under Gemma's tanh soft-cap on saturated edges. This is the only checklist item that bounds removal completeness rather than the win/lose verdict — FRA can win the separability comparison at any matched operating point it can reach.

**Underlying reason:** The whole checklist is one separability statement. The behavior's minimal sufficient cause must be a SEPARABLE PRODUCT TERM u^μ_q·u^ν_k inside one (or a few) heads' score functions. When it is: (i) the FRA edit's support is exactly the conjunction's support and its only causal channel is that head's softmax, so its collateral scales with reuse(conjunction) — which is ~0 when the conjunction is the malicious/target-specific thing; (ii) ANY linear residual steer, however gated, must either suppress a marginal endpoint (its score effect is confined to a row/column of the bilinear form — it changes the query's affinity to every key sharing content along one fixed direction) or write into the residual stream where all heads' Q/K/V, the MLP, and every later layer read it — so its collateral scales with reuse(marginal endpoint) × (number of residual readers). The win ratio at matched removal is therefore A ≈ reuse(marginal)/reuse(conjunction), which is huge exactly when both endpoints are COMMON content but their pairing is rare-and-targeted. CCF certifies the cause lives in score space; LBNR certifies the conjunction is the unique carrier (no backup path re-creates the product term); direct-consumption certifies the product term's output IS the behavior; reuse certifies the denominator is small and the numerator large; reach certifies FRA's basis can express enough of the term to act on it. Each failure mode below is the negation of exactly one factor.

**Failure modes:**
- OUTPUT-DIRECTION ROUTING (weight-baked sleeper, EM): the payload is written through OV/unembedding as a residual DIRECTION with near-zero legitimate reuse; there is no score-space cause, and DoM/SVD on the marginal direction is already surgical — FRA has no bilinear gap to exploit (negation of CCF + reuse).
- REDUNDANT / SELF-REPAIRING CIRCUITS (IOI name-movers R=−0.39, docstring R=0.08): cutting the edge triggers backup heads that restore — or overshoot — the behavior; the conjunction is not the unique carrier, so the edit is precise but behaviorally inert (negation of LBNR).
- DIRECTION-ROUTED IN-CONTEXT BEHAVIOR (many-shot jailbreak, binding): the behavior is carried by a distributed ICL task-direction across many weak edges, not a separable pair; worse, the injected behavior can be representationally identical to a legitimate one, so NO method removes it cleanly — there is no separable link to cut (negation of edge-routing + LBNR).
- DOWNSTREAM NONLINEAR CONSUMPTION (greater-than): the QK edge is real and content-conjunctive, but the behavior is computed by an MLP from the attended information; cutting the edge controls the attending, not the computation (negation of direct-consumption).
- CONJUNCTION COLLISION (acronym 'Unit'; poisoned content identical to legit content): the target conjunction's support overlaps legitimate instances of the SAME pair; FRA's content-addressing correctly fires on them too — collateral by design, and A collapses because reuse(conjunction) ≈ reuse(marginal) (negation of the reuse gap).
- REACH CEILING (Gemma 1/4→3/4 backdoors at 16k→65k; 1M regression; GPT-2 LN-centering): SAE basis too coarse, or too fine for the top-K budget, or LN-folding drops centering, or the soft-cap compresses edits on saturated edges — FRA cannot hit the matched-removal operating point, so the win is capped or undefined even though the separability property holds for what it can remove (negation of reach).
- POSITIONAL / SINK EDGE (BOS, lost-in-the-middle effects): the edge is selected by position, not content (CCF=0); a position-patch is the correct tool and FRA's content-addressing adds nothing (negation of edge-routing's content clause).

## Synthetic settings
- **Induction cue-suppression (gpt2-small, anchor)** (known): One known bilinear gate — (query=X) × (key=after-previous-X) — with no semantics, no MLP involvement, and head redundancy that is enumerable (hit all 5 heads); the 15×/235× double-gate locality result is the program's expressivity anchor. — tests: Pure-conjunction expressivity: that a rank-1 cell edit suppresses an association at near-zero off-conjunction support (the support-not-rigged and faithful-scale c≈2 checks).
- **In-context trigger→payload backdoor (gpt2 + gemma, anchor)** (known): Trigger and payload are NORMAL tokens planted in the prompt, so marginal-reuse is high by construction while conjunction-reuse is zero; the same task FRA lost weight-baked, so the ranking flip (FRA 0.07 nats vs DoM 1.83 / conv-SAE 6.06) isolates WHERE the association lives as the only changed variable. — tests: The marginal≫conjunction reuse bound — the separability ratio's numerator — against the exact baselines that win the direction-routed case.
- **Two-needle key-value retrieval (gemma-2-2b, anchor)** (known): Two needles sharing structure force pair-specificity (random off-edge null = base); RMSNorm makes the FRA correction exact (ratio 1.07), removing the reconstruction confound; the confirmed ~1100× vs content-gated projection-removal steer is the corrected-headline-metric anchor. — tests: The decisive separability claim itself (legit-content KL at matched removal vs the strongest fair baseline) under exact normalization.
- **Conjunction-collision dial** (NEW): Plant one trigger→payload pair, then add N LEGITIMATE occurrences of the SAME query-content×key-content pair in the held-out eval text, sweeping N from 0 (the current backdoor setting) to dense; everything else held fixed, so reuse(conjunction)/reuse(marginal) is the only moving variable. — tests: Failure mode 5 (conjunction collision) quantitatively — predicts the separability advantage A decays as conjunction-reuse approaches marginal-reuse, turning the validated win/lose predictor into the MAGNITUDE LAW the program currently lacks (A ≈ reuse(marginal)/reuse(conjunction)).
- **Redundancy dial (synthetic backup circuit)** (NEW): Carry the planted association over R parallel routes — duplicate demonstrations at different offsets so distinct induction edges (and layers) each suffice — so LBNR-R can be degraded continuously and measured exactly, unlike natural backups (IOI) which are all-or-nothing and uncontrolled. — tests: The LBNR bound as a threshold: at what redundancy does edge-cut R fall below the win boundary, and does cutting the UNION of routes (FRA can address all R conjunctions at once, a position-patch cannot) restore the win? Stresses whether LBNR is about the EDGE being unique or the CONJUNCTION being unique.
- **MLP-interposition probe** (NEW): Plant an in-context association whose payload is not the attended token but a fixed FUNCTION of it computed downstream (e.g., attend to a digit, emit its successor via the known successor circuitry): the QK edge is identical to the winning backdoor case, only the consumption changes, isolating the direct-consumption bound from everything else. — tests: Bound 3 (direct consumption / the greater-than failure mode) as a controlled dissociation: FRA should cut attendance fully while behavioral removal degrades exactly with how much the MLP re-derives the answer from residual leftovers.
- **Granularity × top-K reach law** (NEW): One fixed in-context backdoor on gemma, sweep GemmaScope width {16k, 65k, +1M-mix} × K ∈ {4…256} pairs: the 65k>16k gain and the 1M regression at fixed K=12 are existing single points; the full surface separates 'SAE explains more of the edge' (norm-recovery, flat) from 'features fragment' (top-K capture, the real driver). — tests: The reach bound: reach = f(width, K) with the prediction that the optimum K grows with width (fragmentation, not reconstruction, limits removal) — and that separability is reach-independent at every attainable matched operating point.
- **Soft-cap saturation stress** (NEW): Same planted edge at controlled baseline score margins — push the pre-cap score into and out of the 50·tanh(s/50) regime on gemma (e.g., by stacking demonstrations) with uncapped gpt2 as the contrast arm; tanh'(s/50) gives a parameter-free quantitative prediction for the edit attenuation. — tests: The soft-cap clause of the reach bound: edit-effectiveness on the pattern should track tanh'(s/50), confirming the cap (not the SAE) as Gemma's marginal reach limiter on saturated edges.
- **Shared-endpoint MQAR deletion (multi-query associative recall, Arora et al. 2023 style)** (NEW): A context of key→value pairs where the SAME value token appears under two different keys and the SAME key-type queries multiple values: delete exactly one key→value association while every endpoint stays in active legitimate use inside the SAME prompt — the maximal-stress version of separability, on an established synthetic benchmark from the ICL/SSM literature. — tests: The underlying separability reason in its purest form: FRA should delete one cell while sibling associations sharing both endpoints survive untouched; any direction/content-gated steer must damage the siblings (its score effect occupies the whole row/column of the bilinear form).
