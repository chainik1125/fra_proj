\begin{abstract}
We carry the feature-resolved attention (FRA) programme into the
constrained-belief-update setting of \emph{Piotrowski et al.}\ (\Ptwo) and
\emph{Tahir} (\Pthree). Working in \Pthree's model class and \Ptwo's softmax
instantiation, and importing the QK gauge theorem and OV path calculus of the two
prior-sprint notes (\priorA, \priorB), we ask which FRA objects are computation
and which are gauge, and whether every attribution and every edit can be predicted
\emph{ex ante} from the constrained-belief theory. The answer, established by
exact enumeration over all $3^{10}=59049$ length-$10$ Mess3 sequences, is sharp.
The FRA-QK (score) side is a \emph{gauge coordinate}: at a constrained-belief
optimum the token$\times$token score block is additively separable and consists
entirely of gauge coordinates, and on the faithful \Ptwo\ model a loss-preserving
weight dial (bit-identical behaviour, max logit change $7\!\times\!10^{-7}$) swings
a fixed FRA-QK cut's cross-entropy effect by $140\times$, with the minimum sitting
exactly where a measured pedestal crosses zero and an irreducible residue at the
$\sim\!10^{-5}$-nat scale the loss never sees. The FRA-OV (transported content)
side is the real handle: the per-(token, lag) attribution is $\zeta^{d-s}\gb(z)$ up
to a softmax-forced normalization warp $\lambda_d=1/(1-\zeta^d)$; severing a
token's channel drives its belief-tracking linearly to zero at $c^\ast=1/\rho$,
with the slope $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ predicted from clean-model statistics alone to
\emph{four significant figures} on all five measured channels, and collapses the
intermediate fractal along $\gb(z)$. The sparse dictionary that names the features
is itself the binding constraint: a TopK SAE learns a minimal contrast code in
which one of three token channels does not exist as a cut set. For the
negative-eigenvalue case only the head-sum
$\sum_h\Ab^{(h)}_{d,s}\Vb^{(h)}\eb_{z_s}=\zeta^{d-s}\gb(z_s)$ is loss-pinned;
per-head attributions are a gauge family \emph{not} freely realizable under softmax
($O(\varepsilon)$ generic, $O(1)$ at parity-empty rows). Every claim carries an
honesty tag; we close with the bridge to \Pone's factored world and an honesty
ledger.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{What feature-resolved attention is}

Fix the one-layer attention circuit of \Pthree\ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:transformer}
below): the destination residual receives a \emph{direct} contribution
$\Wu\We\xb_d$ and an \emph{attention-mediated} contribution
$\sum_{s\le d}\Ab_{ds}\Wu\Wov\We\xb_s$. Feature-resolved attention (FRA) is the
decomposition of the two attention-internal objects in a chosen feature basis of
the residual stream:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{FRA-QK} resolves the pre-softmax score $\sigma(d,s)=\qb_d\cdot\kb_s$
into \emph{feature $\times$ feature} terms: which query feature of the destination
reads which key feature of the source. In a basis $\xb_s=\eb(z_s)+\pb_s$ (token
feature plus positional feature) the score splits exactly into four blocks
(pos$\times$pos, pos$\times$tok, tok$\times$pos, tok$\times$tok); the
token$\times$token block is the FRA-QK \emph{feature-content} score.
\item \textbf{FRA-OV} resolves the transported content $\Ab_{ds}\Wu\Wov\We\xb_s$ by
\emph{source feature and lag}: which feature, copied from how many tokens back, is
written into the destination. It factorizes as (a scalar lag profile) $\times$ (a
token-indexed content direction).
\end{itemize}
An FRA \emph{attribution} reads a coefficient off one of these resolved objects; an
FRA \emph{edit} (or \emph{cut}) removes a named feature-term at a gain $c$ ($c=1$ is
exact severing, $c>1$ counter-injects) and asks what the model then does. The
programme's central question is whether these attributions and edits measure the
network's \emph{computation} or merely a \emph{gauge} choice --- a coordinate that a
loss-preserving reparameterization can move at will.

\subsection{The question, in \Ptwo's and \Pthree's own world}

\Ptwo\ shows that a one-layer transformer trained on a single Mess3 process
represents, at the post-attention residual, the \emph{constrained belief update}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cbu-intro}
\rb_1(d)=\pib+\sum_{s=1}^{d}\zeta^{\,d-s}\gb(z_s),
\end{equation}
a lag-weighted sum of per-token displacement vectors $\gb(z_s)$ in a
two-dimensional belief plane, with a single spectral rate $\zeta$. \Pthree\ derives,
for the frozen-attention MSE surrogate, the optimal $\Wb=\Wu\We$,
$\Vb=\Wu\Wov\We$ and the exact loss symmetry $\Wb\to\Wb+\ub\one^\top$,
$\Vb\to\Vb-\ub\one^\top$. The prior sprint built, on a separate toy platform, a
theory of FRA: a QK gauge theorem (which score edits are gauge) and an OV path
calculus (which content edits obey a tracking law). This note asks the concrete
question:
\begin{quote}\itshape
In \Ptwo's exact setting, which FRA objects are computation and which are gauge,
and can every attribution and every edit be predicted ex ante from the
constrained-belief theory?
\end{quote}
The headline, established by exact enumeration (no sampling error anywhere in
Phase~1):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{FRA-QK is gauge} (\S\ref{sec:qk}). At a constrained-belief optimum
the token$\times$token score block is additively separable and consists entirely
of gauge coordinates; its measured cut effect on the faithful \Ptwo\ model is a
$140\times$ V-shape as a loss-preserving dial is turned, minimized where a measured
pedestal crosses zero, with an irreducible residue at the noise floor the loss
never sees.
\item \textbf{FRA-OV is the handle} (\S\ref{sec:ov}). The transported-content
attributions are the constrained-belief per-source terms; severing a token's
channel removes exactly the predicted content, tracking response is linear in the
gain, and the \emph{ex-ante} slope $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ --- from clean-model
statistics only --- matches the measured tracking null to four significant figures
on all five channels.
\item \textbf{The dictionary is the binding constraint} (\S\ref{sec:dict}). A TopK
SAE learns a minimal contrast code in which one of three token channels does not
exist as a latent set; every intervention number remains predictable, but only
through the code's mixing matrix.
\item \textbf{The two-head hinge} (\S\ref{sec:twohead}). For $\zeta<0$ only the
head-sum attribution is loss-pinned; the per-head decomposition is a gauge family
that is \emph{not} freely realizable under softmax, obstructing clean single-gain
interventions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Setting}\label{sec:setting}

\subsection{The model class (\Pthree)}

We study \Pthree's one-head attention-only predictor
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transformer}
f(\Xb)_{t,:}=\Wu\We\,\xb_t+\sum_{s\le t}\Wu\,\Ab_{ts}\,\Wov\We\,\xb_s,
\qquad f(\Xb)=\Wb\Xb+\Vb\,\Xb\Ab^\top,
\end{equation}
with effective matrices $\Wb=\Wu\We$ (direct path) and $\Vb=\Wu\Wov\We$ (context
path) and $\Ab$ a causal row-stochastic matrix. \Pthree\ analyses the
frozen-attention optimum under the MSE surrogate and establishes the
$\vocab$-dimensional loss symmetry
\begin{equation}\label{eq:p3-symmetry}
\Wb\to\Wb+\ub\one_{\vocab}^\top,\qquad \Vb\to\Vb-\ub\one_{\vocab}^\top
\qquad(\ub\in\mathbb{R}^{\vocab}\ \text{arbitrary}),
\end{equation}
the ambient degeneracy that, resolved in a feature basis, becomes the FRA-QK gauge
of \S\ref{sec:qk} and the $k{=}0$ skip/diagonal split of \S\ref{sec:ideal}. \Ptwo\
trains this class with softmax attention and CE loss; we keep \Pthree's
function-space vocabulary and add the softmax scores only where FRA-QK needs them.

\subsection{The \Ptwo\ softmax instantiation (App.\ B, faithful)}

We reproduce \Ptwo's App.-B configuration exactly (\texttt{common.py}): a
TransformerLens \texttt{HookedTransformer}, \texttt{n\_layers}=1,
$d_{\mathrm{model}}=64$, $d_{\mathrm{mlp}}=256$, GELU, LayerNorm, causal attention,
$d_{\mathrm{vocab}}=3$, \emph{no} BOS, learned additive positional embeddings,
context length $L=N_{\mathrm{ctx}}=10$, cross-entropy loss, Adam ($10^{-4}$, no
weight decay), ${\approx}15$M tokens. Two faithful configurations:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Config A}: $x=0.15,\ \alpha=0.6,\ \zeta=1-3x=+0.55$, one head
($d_{\mathrm{head}}=64$).
\item \textbf{Config B}: $x=0.50,\ \alpha=0.6,\ \zeta=1-3x=-0.50$, two heads
($d_{\mathrm{head}}=32$), anti-parallel OVs.
\end{itemize}
The residual at source $s$ is $\xb_s=\eb(z_s)+\pb_s$; the pattern is
$\Ab_{d,s}=\softmax{\sigma(d,\cdot)}_s$ with bilinear scores
$\sigma(d,s)=(\Wq\phi(\xb_d)+\boldsymbol b_Q)\cdot(\Wk\phi(\xb_s)+\boldsymbol b_K)/\sqrt{d_h}$,
value $\vb_s=\Wov\xb_s$, and frozen-LN map $\phi$. The belief geometry lives at the
post-attention, pre-MLP residual ($\resmid$, \Ptwo's $x_d^{(\mathrm{mid})}$),
$\xb_d^{\mathrm{mid}}=\xb_d+\cb_d$, $\cb_d=\sum_{s\le d}\Ab_{d,s}\vb_s$, read out by
a \emph{linear} map $\Rb:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\Pcal$ to the belief plane. The MLP
performs the subsequent nonlinear warp from the constrained fractal to the full
Bayesian geometry (\Ptwo, ``Post-MLP''); it is outside the FRA-of-attention
analysis and not modeled here. LayerNorm is treated as a frozen,
content-independent per-position linear scale $\Phi$ (imported Assumption (A2)); the
residual token-dependent LN leak is absorbed into the measurable \emph{content
leak} $\varepsilon$ (imported Definition, \priorA).

\subsection{Exact-enumeration methodology}\label{sec:enum}

\begin{remark}[the analysis distribution is exact]\label{rem:exact}
Every Phase-1 number is \emph{exact}. The analysis set is all
$\vocab^{L}=3^{10}=59049$ length-$L$ input sequences weighted by their exact

\end{remark}

\subsection{The Mess3 process algebra}\label{sec:mess3}

Token-labeled transition operators (standard convention, \texttt{mess3.py}) are
$\Tx_{ij}=t(i,j)\,e(x\mid j)$ with $t=(1-2x)\Ib+x(\one\one^\top-\Ib)$ and
$e(x\mid j)=\alpha$ if $x=j$ else $(1-\alpha)/2$. All of the following are
\htag{exact} (checked in \texttt{mess3.py}): $\Tb:=\sum_x\Tx=t$ is row-stochastic
with stationary $\pib=(\tfrac13,\tfrac13,\tfrac13)$ and eigenvalues
$\{1,\zeta,\zeta\}$, $\zeta=1-3x$ doubly degenerate; $P(x)=\pib\Tx\one=\tfrac13$;
the conditional transition $\Tcond{x}:=\Tx/P(x)$. The load-bearing object is the
\emph{token displacement}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gdef}
\gb(x):=\pib\,\Tcond{x}-\pib\ \in\ \mathbb{R}^3,\qquad
\gb(x)\cdot\one=0,\qquad \textstyle\sum_x\gb(x)=0.
\end{equation}
The spectral projectors are $\Tb=\Tb_1+\zeta\,\Pb_\zeta$ with $\Tb_1=\one\pib$ and
$\Pb_\zeta=\Ib-\one\pib$ ($\Pb_\zeta\one=0$, $\pib\Pb_\zeta=0$,
$\Pb_\zeta^2=\Pb_\zeta$). The \emph{belief plane}
$\Pcal:=\{\vb\in\mathbb{R}^3:\vb\cdot\one=0\}$ has $\dim\Pcal=2$; all three $\gb(x)$
lie in $\Pcal$ and (since $\sum_x\gb(x)=0$) span it, so any two are a basis, and
$\Pcal=\mathrm{im}(\Pb_\zeta)$.

\begin{lemma}[spectral identity]\label{lem:spec}
\htag{exact} For every token $x$ and integer $n\ge0$,
$\pib\,\Tcond{x}\,\Tb^{\,n}-\pib=\zeta^{\,n}\gb(x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
$\pib\Tcond{x}=\pib+\gb(x)$ by \eqref{eq:gdef} and $\pib\Tb^n=\pib$, so the left
side is $\gb(x)\Tb^n$. Now $\gb(x)\cdot(\one\pib)=(\gb(x)\cdot\one)\pib=0$, so
$\gb(x)\Tb=\zeta\,\gb(x)\Pb_\zeta=\zeta\,\gb(x)$, whence
$\gb(x)\Tb^n=\zeta^n\gb(x)$.
\end{proof}

Summing the per-source terms gives the constrained belief update in spectral form
(\Ptwo's spectral constrained-belief equation, instantiated), i.e.\
Eq.~\eqref{eq:cbu-intro}:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cbu}
\rb_1(z_{1:d})=\pib+\ub_d,\qquad
\ub_d:=\sum_{s=1}^{d}\bigl(\pib\Tcond{z_s}\Tb^{\,d-s}-\pib\bigr)
\overset{\text{Lem.\,\ref{lem:spec}}}{=}\sum_{s=1}^{d}\zeta^{\,d-s}\gb(z_s)\ \in\ \Pcal.
\end{equation}
All lag-dependence sits in the scalar $\zeta^{d-s}$; all token-dependence in the
plane vector $\gb(z_s)$. This factorization is what makes FRA tractable and, as
\S\ref{sec:qk} shows, what puts Mess3 on the gauge side of the separability
boundary. Numerically,
$\max_x\max_{n\le5}\|(\pib\Tcond{x}\Tb^n-\pib)-\zeta^n\gb(x)\|_\infty=1.1\times10^{-16}$.%
\footnote{\texttt{mess3.py} self-check; $x=0.15,\alpha=0.6,\zeta=0.55$.}

\subsection{The bridge lemma: \Pthree's token statistics carry \Ptwo's belief vectors}
\label{sec:bridge}

The formal link between \Pthree's MSE-optimal effective parameters and \Ptwo's
belief-plane displacements is a column identity for \Pthree's spectral
skip-bigrams $\Mb_\lambda$ (with
$(\Mb_\lambda)_{ij}=\pib\Tb^{(j)}\Pb_\lambda\Tb^{(i)}\one$), imported verbatim from
\priorA.

\begin{lemma}[bridge; imported \priorA]\label{lem:bridge}
\htag{exact} For every nonstationary eigenvalue $\lambda\neq1$,
$\Mb_\lambda\,\eb_j=p_j\,\Wu\bigl(\gb(j)\Pb_\lambda\bigr)^{\!\top}$, where $\Wu$ is
\Pthree's unembedding (rows $\one^\top(\Tb^{(i)})^\top$) and $p_j$ the stationary
probability of token $j$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}[Proof (imported)]
$(\Mb_\lambda)_{ij}=\pib\Tb^{(j)}\Pb_\lambda\Tb^{(i)}\one$; write
$\pib\Tb^{(j)}=p_j(\pib+\gb(j))$ and use $\pib\Pb_\lambda=0$ for $\lambda\neq1$ to
get $(\Mb_\lambda)_{ij}=p_j\,\gb(j)\Pb_\lambda\Tb^{(i)}\one
=p_j\bigl(\Wu(\gb(j)\Pb_\lambda)^\top\bigr)_i$.
\end{proof}

Column $j$ of $\Mb_\lambda$ is $p_j$ times the unembedded $\lambda$-component of
token $j$'s belief displacement: \Pthree's token-space regression statistics are, in
each nonstationary block, exactly \Ptwo's $\gb(j)$-vectors. This is the bridge that
gives the FRA-OV attribution its $\gb(z)$-shape (\S\ref{sec:ov}).

\section{The \Ptwo\ ideal configuration}\label{sec:ideal}

\subsection{Ideal feature basis and pattern}\label{sec:idealbasis}

Imported Assumption (A1) posits the exact residual split $\xb_s=\eb(z_s)+\pb_s$;
\Ptwo's empirical ``embeddings $\parallel$ OV vectors $\propto\gb(z)$'' is posited
as the \emph{ideal embedding} $\eb(z)=C_e\hat\gb(z)$ (a fixed linear lift
$\Pcal\hookrightarrow\mathbb{R}^{d_{\mathrm{model}}}$), so the linear readout obeys
$\Rb\,\eb(z)=C_e\gb(z)$ on the query/direct side and $\Rb\Wov\eb(z)=C_v\gb(z)$ on the
value side (\Ptwo's \texttt{fvn}: all OV vectors of a token are parallel to
$\gb(z)$). \htag{exact given ansatz}

\begin{definition}[\Ptwo\ ideal pattern]\label{def:pattern}
The pure-geometric, row-normalized lag profile is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pattern}
\Ab_{d,s}=\frac{\zeta^{\,d-s}}{N_d},\qquad
N_d=\sum_{s'=1}^{d}\zeta^{\,d-s'}=\frac{1-\zeta^{\,d}}{1-\zeta}\quad(1\le s\le d).
\end{equation}
This is \emph{lag-only with $d$-dependent normalization} in the imported
pattern-class sense; it is the normalizer entering the belief-cut formula
\eqref{eq:beliefcut}.
\end{definition}

\begin{prop}[realizability of the geometric profile; imported \priorA]\label{prop:realize}
\htag{exact for $\zeta\in(0,1)$} The pattern \eqref{eq:pattern} is produced by a
single softmax head with key-side-only, position-linear scores
$\sigma(d,s)=s(-\ln\zeta)+\mathrm{const}(d)$, i.e.\ $k_P(s)=s|\ln\zeta|\,\hat u+w$
(recency slope on the key side). For $\zeta<0$, $\ln\zeta$ is undefined and no
single softmax head realizes it (patterns are nonnegative); two heads split
even/odd lags (\S\ref{sec:tworealize}). Verified: softmax of $\sigma(s)=s(-\ln\zeta)$
reproduces \eqref{eq:pattern} to $1.7\times10^{-16}$.
\end{prop}

\subsection{The lag-0 split: diagonal attention vs skip (the $k{=}0$ gauge)}\label{sec:k0}

Grouping the $s=d$ (lag-0) term of $\ub_d$ in \eqref{eq:cbu}, \Ptwo's
diagonal-attention identity (\Ptwo\ Eq.~\texttt{DiagonalAttention}), instantiated,
is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:k0}
\underbrace{\Rb\,\xb_d^{\mathrm{(pre)}}}_{\text{skip}}
+\underbrace{\Ab_{d,d}\,\Rb\vb_d}_{\text{diagonal attention}}=\gb(z_d),
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the current-token displacement $\gb(z_d)$ is \emph{split} between the residual
skip connection and the diagonal attention, and \emph{only the sum is pinned by the
loss}. This is precisely \Pone's $k{=}0$ degree of freedom (\Pone\ Eq.~28): $\xb_d$
is itself a regressor, so diagonal-attention mass is collinear with the direct
path. Write the \emph{diagonal share}
$a_0(d):=\bigl[\Ab_{d,d}\Rb\vb_d/\gb(z_d)\bigr]_{\text{along }\gb(z_d)}\in[0,1]$;
$a_0=1$ puts all of $\gb(z_d)$ on diagonal attention (forced by one-hot-frozen
embeddings, \Pone), $a_0=0$ puts all of it on the skip. $a_0(d)$ is \emph{pure
gauge}: unidentifiable from the ideal theory, measurable ex post as $\Ab_{d,d}$
times the OV image of the current token. It is the reason the attention-mediated
path-share $\rho<1$ in \S\ref{sec:ov} (an OV cut removes the diagonal, not the
skip). For \eqref{eq:pattern}, $\Ab_{d,d}=1/N_d=(1-\zeta)/(1-\zeta^d)$, shrinking
from $1$ at $d=1$ toward $1-\zeta$.

\subsection{Ideal-ansatz vs softmax-realizable: the normalization warp}\label{sec:warp}

This is the single most important honesty point, and it is where \priorB's
``Finding 5'' corrects a naive reading of \Ptwo.

\begin{prop}[normalization warp; imported \priorB, re-derived]\label{prop:warp}
\htag{exact given the ansatz; realizability correction exact} With additive
positional embeddings the value $\vb_s=\Wov(\eb(z_s)+\pb_s)$ has a
\emph{position-independent} token part, so $\Rb\vb_s=C_v\gb(z_s)+\hb_s$ with $\hb_s$
token-independent. There is then \emph{no} score assignment realizing
$\Ab_{d,s}\Rb\vb_s=\zeta^{d-s}\gb(z_s)$ for all $d$. Concretely, with the pattern
\eqref{eq:pattern} and position-independent values the head output is a
token-independent multiplicative warp of the target,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:warp}
\Rb\cb_d=\sum_{s\le d}\Ab_{d,s}\,C_v\gb(z_s)=\frac{C_v}{N_d}\ub_d=\lambda_d\,\ub_d,
\qquad \boxed{\ \lambda_d=\frac{C_v(1-\zeta)}{1-\zeta^{\,d}}\ }.
\end{equation}
The unique $C_v$ making $\lambda_d\to1$ as $d\to\infty$ is $C_v=1/(1-\zeta)$, giving
$\lambda_d=1/(1-\zeta^d)\ge1$: a geometric overshoot $\zeta^d/(1-\zeta^d)$, largest
at small $d$.
\end{prop}

This is the exact quantitative content of \Ptwo's \emph{unexplained} ``scalar
discrepancy in the first two embedding vectors'': the exact-update reading requires
per-position value magnitudes an additive positional embedding cannot supply.
Verified by direct construction to $2.2\times10^{-16}$: for $C_v=1/(1-\zeta)$ the
first five destinations give
$\lambda_d=(2.222,1.434,1.200,1.101,1.053)\to1/(1-\zeta^d)$.

\begin{prop}[the exact-update alternative is not \Ptwo-realizable; imported \priorB]\label{prop:sink}
\htag{exact given ansatz} One \emph{can} hit $\ub_d$ exactly with a \emph{sink
pattern} $\Ab_{d,1}=\zeta^{d-1}$, $\Ab_{d,s}=(1-\zeta)\zeta^{d-s}$ for $s\ge2$
(position 1 is the BOS-less attention sink absorbing the excess mass
$\zeta^{d-1}$), together with position-dependent value magnitudes
$\Rb\vb_1=\gb(z_1)$, $\Rb\vb_s=\gb(z_s)/(1-\zeta)$. But a token-independent additive
$\hb_s$ cannot produce a token-dependent multiplicative $1/(1-\zeta)$, so this
realization is \emph{outside} the \Ptwo\ architecture. It is a ``what it would take''
reference, and it reproduces \Ptwo's anomalously-large $\Ab_{2,1}$ /
anomalously-small $\vb_1$ observations exactly (head output $=\ub_d$ to
$1.1\times10^{-16}$).
\end{prop}

\subsection{The negative-$\zeta$ two-head realization (Config B)}\label{sec:tworealize}

For $\zeta<0$ the target per-source coefficient $\zeta^{d-s}=(-1)^{d-s}|\zeta|^{d-s}$
oscillates in sign, but softmax patterns are nonnegative. \Ptwo's resolution (\Ptwo\
Eq.~\texttt{Ads\_2head\_relation}): \emph{two heads}, head 1 carrying even lags,
head 2 odd lags, with \emph{anti-parallel OVs}
$\widehat{\Rb\vb^{(1)}_s}=-\widehat{\Rb\vb^{(2)}_s}$, both $\propto\gb(z_s)$, and the
head-sum reproducing the oscillating coefficient,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:twohead-realize}
\sum_{h=1,2}\Ab^{(h)}_{d,s}\Rb\vb^{(h)}_s=(-1)^{d-s}|\zeta|^{\,d-s}\gb(z_s)
=\zeta^{\,d-s}\gb(z_s).
\end{equation}
Each head has its own non-negative pattern supported on its parity class,
normalized \emph{separately} by softmax. With $\kappa:=\zeta^2$, the per-head
normalizers are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Ndhead}
N_d^{(1)}=\frac{1-\kappa^{\lceil d/2\rceil}}{1-\kappa},\qquad
N_d^{(2)}=|\zeta|\,\frac{1-\kappa^{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}}{1-\kappa},\qquad
N_d^{(1)}+N_d^{(2)}=\sum_{s\le d}|\zeta|^{d-s}.
\end{equation}
Because each head renormalizes its own rows, a key-side cut acts on the two heads
through \emph{different} normalizers --- the source of the two-head hinge
(\S\ref{sec:twohead}). The warp of \S\ref{sec:warp} applies per head with $N_d$
replaced by $N_d^{(h)}$; the two heads overshoot by \emph{different} factors
($\lambda_2\approx2\lambda_1$), so no single value scale un-warps both.
\htag{exact given ansatz}

\section{FRA-QK in \Ptwo's world}\label{sec:qk}

\subsection{The four score blocks and the gauge coordinates}\label{sec:blocks}

Define the score-side vectors ($1/\sqrt{d_h}$ absorbed, biases folded into the
positional parts) $q_E(z)=\Wq\Phi\,\eb(z)$, $q_P(d)=\Wq\Phi\,\pb_d+b_Q$,
$k_E(z)=\Wk\Phi\,\eb(z)$, $k_P(s)=\Wk\Phi\,\pb_s+b_K$, so $\sigma(d,s)=\qb_d\cdot\kb_s$
splits \emph{exactly} into four blocks and the token$\times$token block
$Q(z,z'):=q_E(z)\cdot k_E(z')$ is the FRA-QK feature$\times$feature score mass. Bars
denote token means, hats centered parts.

\begin{assumption}[premises transfer to \Ptwo]\label{ass:qk}
\htag{A0 exact for Mess3; A1 idealized; A2 error $O(\varepsilon)$}
(A0) full support --- every (token, position) and every ordered token pair at any
two positions occurs (Mess3: every finite string has positive probability); (A1)
exact split $\xb_s=\eb(z_s)+\pb_s$; (A2) content-independent frozen-LN scale, the
residual token-dependent LN leak absorbed into the content leak
$\varepsilon:=\max_{d,z,z'}|\qb_d\cdot(k_E(z)-k_E(z'))|
+\max_{z,s,s'}|\hat q_E(z)\cdot(k_P(s)-k_P(s'))|$.
Crucially, the ideal pattern \eqref{eq:pattern} is token-independent (depends only on
$(d,s)$), so Conjecture Z1 (lag-only optimality) is \emph{assumed satisfied by
construction} here; everything below is \htag{exact given a token-independent-pattern
configuration}, the imported theorem's exactness class.
\end{assumption}

\begin{theorem}[characterization; imported \priorA, instantiated]\label{thm:char}
\htag{exact given Assumption~\ref{ass:qk}} The pattern is token-independent on the
support for all $d\ge2$ iff \textbf{(C1)} $\qb_d\cdot\hat k_E(z)=0$ for every
realized query and every $z$ (including the diagonal token) and \textbf{(C2)}
$\hat q_E(z)\cdot\hat k_P(s)=0$ for every $z$ and realized $s$. Differencing (C1)
over $z_d$ gives \textbf{(C1a)} $\hat q_E(z)\cdot\hat k_E(z')=0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{prop}[the feature$\times$feature mass is pure gauge; imported \priorA]\label{prop:featfeat}
\htag{exact given a token-independent pattern} Under (C1)--(C2),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Qsplit}
Q(z_d,z_s)=\underbrace{\bar q_E\cdot\bar k_E}_{\text{const}}
+\underbrace{\hat q_E(z_d)\cdot\bar k_E}_{\text{query-token gauge}}
+\underbrace{\bar q_E\cdot\hat k_E(z_s)}_{\text{key-token gauge }b(z_s)}
+\underbrace{\hat q_E(z_d)\cdot\hat k_E(z_s)}_{=\,0\text{ by (C1a)}}.
\end{equation}
Every token-dependent term is additively separable and is a coordinate on the gauge
orbit spanned by the \emph{pedestal} $\bped(d):=\qb_d\cdot\bar k_E$ (coefficient of
every key-side cut), the \emph{key read} $b(z):=\bar q_E\cdot\hat k_E(z)$, the
\emph{profile share} $\theta$ (fraction of the recency slope carried on the
token-query side, $\bar q_E\cdot\hat k_P(s)=\theta\,s(-\ln\zeta)$ mod row constant),
and the \emph{row constants} $\rho(z):=\hat q_E(z)\cdot(\bar k_E+\bar k_P)$
(inert: softmax kills row constants). The centered interaction block is
gauge-invariant and, at a token-independent-pattern configuration, identically zero
(C1a); hence the loss level set contains configurations where $Q\equiv0$ and where
$Q$ dominates the score budget, with identical behaviour. \textbf{FRA-QK attribution
here measures gauge, not computation.}
\end{prop}

The gauge families (imported \priorA, Prop.\ gauge) are explicit weight
reparameterizations that move these coordinates through zero to arbitrary magnitude
at fixed pattern, forward pass, and loss: G1 embedding re-split
$\eb(z)\to\eb(z)+u,\ \pb_s\to\pb_s-u$ (exact \emph{everywhere}, on and off
distribution --- the primary dial); G2 per-row-constant query updates; G2$'$
pedestal (key-side); G3 null-direction key updates (exact on distribution). What no
dial can move is the centered interaction $\hat q_E(z)\cdot\hat k_E(z')$; at the
\Ptwo\ optimum it is zero, so \emph{everything} FRA-QK measures is dialable. This is
the imported boundary theorem, branch 1: the \Ptwo\ target kernel
$\zeta^{d-s}\gb(z_s)\Pb_\zeta$ is separable (lag factor token-free, content factor
lag-free), so FRA-QK has zero causal handle at every minimizer --- Mess3 is on the
\emph{null} side of the induction/matching boundary (\priorA's parallelogram lemma).

\subsection{Cut calculus at the \Ptwo\ ideal (Config A)}\label{sec:cutcalc}

\begin{theorem}[cut calculus; imported \priorA, instantiated]\label{thm:cut}
\htag{exact given (C1)--(C2), $\varepsilon=0$; else additive $O(c\varepsilon)$}
With \Ptwo\ values $\Rb\vb_s=C_v\gb(z_s)$ and pattern \eqref{eq:pattern}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Key-side single-token cut of $z^\ast$ at gain $c$.} The subtracted score
is $\bped(d)\,\ind[z_s=z^\ast]$ (pedestal universality: the same $\bped(d)$ for every
token, by (C1)), with belief-plane response the exact two-valued form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:beliefcut}
\Delta\Rb\cb_d=C_v\,\frac{(e^{-c\bped(d)}-1)\,w_{z^\ast}}
{1+(e^{-c\bped(d)}-1)\,w_{z^\ast}}\Bigl(\gb(z^\ast)-\tfrac{\rb_1(d)-\pib}{N_d}\Bigr),
\qquad w_{z^\ast}=\!\!\sum_{s\le d,\,z_s=z^\ast}\!\!\Ab_{d,s}:
\end{equation}
a distortion along $\gb(z^\ast)-\bar\ub_d$ (with $\bar\ub_d=(\rb_1(d)-\pib)/N_d$ the
\emph{normalized} constrained update), scaled by the gauge coordinate $\bped(d)$,
saturating at ``delete all $z^\ast$-keys and renormalize'', and \emph{identically
zero at every gain at the canonical gauge $\bped=0$}. It corrupts the belief-state
(grammar) direction and leaves aggregate content protected. Verified to
$8.9\times10^{-16}$.
\item \emph{Key-side full token set.} Every key carries exactly one active token
feature, so the summed edit is $\delta(d,s)=\bped(d)$ for all $s$: a per-row
constant, \textbf{exactly inert at every gain}. Single features are cuttable only
because the cut breaks this sum. With leak, $\mathrm{osc}(\delta)\le\varepsilon$,
effect $O(c\varepsilon)$. Verified $2.2\times10^{-16}$.
\item \emph{Query-side cut of $z^\ast$.} Only rows with $z_d=z^\ast$ are affected,
$\delta(d,s)=\rho(z^\ast)+b(z_s)+\chi(s)$; the constant is inert, the key-token piece
$b(z_s)$ is the same function for every cut token (by (C1a)), and when the gauge
routes $\chi(s)=\theta\,s(-\ln\zeta)$ the affected rows' decay rate morphs
\begin{equation}\label{eq:morph}
\zeta\;\longrightarrow\;\zeta^{\,1-c\theta}\qquad\text{for rows with }z_d=z^\ast,
\end{equation}
exactly and at all orders (uniform at $c\theta=1$, reversed beyond); inert at the
canonical gauge $\theta=0,\,b\equiv0$. Verified $1.7\times10^{-16}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

diagonal share. \htag{explained qualitatively (warp $\times$ shrinkage), not
decomposed}

\subsection{Quadratic CE with no null; forced collateral}\label{sec:collateral}

\paragraph{Quadratic CE, no behavioural null.} The exact severing CE is
$\mathrm{CE}(c)=\mathrm{CE}_0+c\,\ell_1+\tfrac{c^2}{2}\ell_2$; for $x=0.15$ the
constrained predictor is nearly Bayes-optimal (gap $7\times10^{-5}$ nats), so
$\ell_1\approx0$ and the CE vertex sits near $0$ while the tracking null sits at
$1/\rho$ --- two distinct predictions. The one-layer model has no redundant second
path, so ``use'' $\equiv$ ``presence'': there is no gain-tuned behavioural null in
\Ptwo's world (the removal factor $\mathrm{RF}(c)\approx(c\rho)^2$ under concept
purity; a single-token channel is impure, so $c^\ast$ must be read from the tracking
null, not from $\mathrm{RF}(1)$). \htag{measured}

\paragraph{Geometrically forced collateral.} The three displacement directions
$\gb(z)$ span a 2-plane and sum to zero, so no edit can reduce one token's coupling
without increasing the others': cutting $z_0$ boosts $z_1,z_2$ tracking above 1
(token-1 tracking rises to ${\sim}1.5\times$). ``Selective concept removal'' is
impossible here for dimension-counting reasons, before any question of method.
\htag{exact given ansatz; measured}

\begin{corollary}[geometry collapse]\label{cor:geom}
\htag{exact given ansatz; measured} The intermediate fractal
$\{\rb_1(d):\text{prefixes}\}$ collapses along $\gb(z^\ast)$ under the $z^\ast$ cut:
the belief-cloud variance along $\widehat{\gb(z^\ast)}$ drops as $(1-c\rho)^2$ up to a
single-feature impurity floor. On the idealized model the plane covariance eigenvalues
go isotropic $[0.0731,0.0731]\to[0.0098,0.0731]$ ($7.5\times$, $87\%$ removed) at
$c=1$; on the trained checkpoint (oracle $g$-channel, exact $3^{10}$) clean
$[0.097,0.100]\to$ cut-$z_0$ $[0.0158,0.100]$ ($16\%$), $c=2$ $[0.0050,\cdot]$ ($5\%$),
cut-$z_2$ $[0.0214,\cdot]$ ($21\%$): one plane eigenvalue collapses, the orthogonal
one is untouched. The residual $\sim\!16\%$ is the impurity --- the $\gb$'s are
non-orthogonal ($\sum_z\gb=0$), so other tokens' displacements retain a
$\widehat{\gb(z^\ast)}$-component the $z^\ast$-only cut cannot remove; full plane
collapse needs $\ge2$ token-feature cuts.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Lag-window ladder.} Exact-enumeration $\Delta$CE$(c=1)$ for lag-window
cuts: lag-0 $8.09\times10^{-3}$, lag-1 $3.27\times10^{-3}$, lag-2 $5.40\times10^{-4}$,
lags $3$--$9$ $2.74\times10^{-4}$; ratios $0.41,0.17$ bracket $\zeta^2=0.303$ (each
lag carries $\zeta^\tau$ evidence, CE cost quadratic near the optimum), and the tail
is nearly inert. But the $\tau\ge3$ cost \emph{exceeds} the kernel-family prediction
($\approx0$) by $16\times$ because the cut removes the window's \emph{entire} content
(including normalization-pedestal components the MLP relies on), not just its
$\gb$-content: full-content window cuts carry built-in collateral. The
$\gb$-content-only window cut (now run) lands in the ladder-predicted rank order over
$1.5$ decades --- $\Delta$CE $1.69\times10^{-3}/3.14\times10^{-4}/6.6\times10^{-5}$ for
lag-$1$/lag-$2$/lags-$3$--$9$, vs the ladder's
$1.11\times10^{-3}/1.13\times10^{-4}/{-}4.2\times10^{-5}$ --- while the full-content cuts
above are ${\sim}4\times$ larger: the excess is exactly the non-$\gb$ content.%
\footnote{\texttt{out/A/close\_gaps.json}: \texttt{gcontent\_window\_dCE\_c1} vs
\texttt{ladder\_prediction}.}
\htag{measured; collateral confirmed}

\section{The dictionary theorem-of-practice}\label{sec:dict}

The closed forms of \S\ref{sec:ov} presume the analyst can name the token channel
$\gb(z)$. The sparse dictionary that provides the names is itself the binding
constraint, in the cleanest habitat FRA will ever see.%
\footnote{\texttt{out/A/fra\_dict.json}, \texttt{out/A/dict\_deep.json};
\texttt{fra\_dict.py}, \texttt{dict\_deep.py}. resid\_pre is attention's input, a
deterministic function of (token, position) with 30 distinct values.}

\paragraph{A minimal contrast code; a missing channel.} On resid\_pre a TopK SAE (12
latents, $K=3$, FVU $2\times10^{-6}$) reliably learns \emph{9 positional} latents and
\emph{two} token latents for \emph{three} tokens (all three SAE seeds). \textbf{Token
1 is encoded by absence} (+ $\bdec$), so ``sever token 1's channel'' does not exist as
a latent-set operation --- a concrete instance of the single-feature rank-deficiency
of \S\ref{sec:collateral}: the concept is 2-dimensional, the TopK code is minimal. The
oracle $\gb(1)$ channel exists for all three tokens and its cut works
($\Delta$CE $3.46\times10^{-3}$), so the obstruction is the code's minimality, not the
concept's causal reachability. \htag{measured}

\paragraph{Channels carry contrasts, not theory directions.} The latents that exist
decode as contrasts: the summed token-0 feature is $1.01\,\eb(0)-0.93\,\eb(1)$, and its
transported belief content matches the code-predicted mixture $\chi\cdot\gb$ at cos
$0.9992/0.9953$ (vs $0.90/0.82$ against pure $\gb(z)$), where $\chi$ is the mixing
matrix (support-restricted estimate: rows $u_0\approx(1.01,-0.93,0.00)$,
$u_2\approx(0.01,-0.93,1.00)$ in the $\eb$-basis, token 1 the default). For reference the \emph{oracle} centered token content $\eb_c(z)$
is parallel to $\gb(z)$ at cos $1.0000/0.9966/0.9997$. \htag{measured}

\paragraph{Purity is necessary, not sufficient.} Set-level token purity is perfect
($R^2\approx1.0$); purity --- the prior sprint's criterion --- is not the obstruction.
\textbf{Completeness and centering of the code are what FRA edits inherit}: all the
intervention numbers of \S\ref{sec:ov} remain exactly predictable, but only through
$\chi$, which the analyst must extract from the SAE first (this is why the SAE-channel
$\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in the \S\ref{sec:track} table is larger than the oracle's --- the
contrast content plus pedestal). The two-head model ($\zeta<0$) is worse: its
head-summed channel geometry scrambles (per-head OVs anti-parallel, so only per-head or
pattern-weighted objects are meaningful), and even the \emph{number} of token latents
varies across SAE seeds ($4/3/2$). The resid\_mid SAE does not hide the computation:
its error carries \emph{less} belief-plane content ($3.3\%$) than its overall size
($5.9\%$). \htag{measured}

\section{The two-head hinge ($\zeta<0$)}\label{sec:twohead}

\subsection{What the loss pins}\label{sec:hingepin}

For $\zeta=-0.5$ softmax nonnegativity forces two heads with anti-parallel OVs; only
the head-sum is loss-pinned,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:headsum}
\sum_{h=1,2}\Ab^{(h)}_{d,s}\,\Rb\vb^{(h)}_s=\zeta^{\,d-s}\gb(z_s)
=\mathrm{pinned}(\tau)\gb(z_s),\qquad \mathrm{pinned}(\tau):=\zeta^\tau.
\end{equation}
\textbf{The invariant} is this head-summed FRA-OV attribution; per-head attributions
are gauge.

\begin{prop}[per-head gauge family and its softmax obstruction]\label{prop:hinge}
\htag{exact in M1; approximate under softmax} Parametrize head $h$ by a nonnegative
lag profile $a_h(\tau)$ and signed OV gain $\gamma_h$ ($\gamma_1>0>\gamma_2$) with
$\gamma_1a_1(\tau)+\gamma_2a_2(\tau)=\zeta^\tau$; the freedom is a \emph{common
additive profile} $\delta(\tau)\ge0$ added to both heads (plus a magnitude split),
with the canonical (\Ptwo) split $a_1^{\mathrm{can}}(\tau)=|\zeta|^\tau\ind[\tau\text{ even}]$,
$a_2^{\mathrm{can}}(\tau)=|\zeta|^\tau\ind[\tau\text{ odd}]$ the minimal member
($\delta\equiv0$). The family has dimension $L$ (one half-line $\delta(\tau)$ per lag)
plus one magnitude split, and is loss-preserving \emph{exactly} for free
row-stochastic patterns (\Pthree's M1). Under softmax with per-head row normalization
the transformation
$\sigma^{(h)}(\tau)\to\log(e^{\sigma^{(h)}(\tau)}+\delta(\tau))$ (applied identically
to both heads, OV blocks untouched) is only approximately loss-preserving:
$O(\varepsilon)$ on rows where both heads have support (coefficient
$\approx0.19$ per $\varepsilon$, measured), but \emph{$O(1)$} at rows where the common
profile newly activates a head whose parity leaves it keyless --- most sharply at
$d=1$, where a lone new entry renormalizes to $1$ and the head-sum changes by a full
$\gb$-vector ($0.2667$, independent of $\varepsilon$). The only \emph{exact} per-head
dials are head relabeling (with OV sign, verified $0.0$) and the per-head $k{=}0$
gauge; no exact per-head OV-magnitude split exists for $\zeta<0$ (that would need the
two heads to share a pattern, but a shared nonnegative pattern cannot produce the sign
oscillation $\zeta^\tau$).
\end{prop}

\subsection{Measured verification (Config B / Brich)}\label{sec:hingemeas}

We use \textbf{Brich} (rich init, \texttt{init\_range} $0.02$) as the cleanest B
model: token-dependence relstd $0.056$, anti-parallel OV cosine $-0.98/-0.98/-1.0$.%
\footnote{\texttt{out/twohead.json}, \texttt{p2\_checks.json};
\texttt{scratchpad/verify\_2head.py}, exact $3^{10}$.}

\paragraph{Head-sum recovers the oscillation; per-head is single-signed.} On Brich,
each head's effective subspace attention is single-signed (OV along $+\gb$ or $-\gb$),
so the sign oscillation cannot live in either head; the head-sum recovers the signed
$\zeta^{d-s}$ kernel --- cosine $0.985$ on the exact row-$8$ per-cell measurement, and
$0.994$ across training on the lag-$1$--$7$ profile estimator (\texttt{twohead.json}'s
\texttt{headsum\_cos\_zeta\_lags1\_7}; lag 0 excluded, as the skip/diagonal split
depresses it).
\htag{confirms \eqref{eq:headsum}}

\paragraph{The invariant locks on; per-head keeps drifting at flat loss.} Across
training (checkpoints every 500 steps, all three B seeds), the head-sum cosine with
$\zeta^\tau$ (lags $1$--$7$) converges --- Brich $-0.169\to0.986$ (mid-training)
$\to0.994$ (final); B $0.930$, B2 $0.875$ at the end --- while the per-head parity
purity \emph{keeps rising} at nearly constant CE (head-1 even-lag fraction
$0.59\to0.71$ over the final $7$k steps). A
controlled $\delta$-dial confirms the family is a low-loss direction: per-head
$|\Delta\alpha|$ up to $0.23$ at $\Delta$CE $\le1.5\times10^{-4}$ (an order below the
$\sim\!10^{-3}$ loss scale, and \emph{slightly negative} at small $\delta$).
\htag{confirms Prop.~\ref{prop:hinge}}

\paragraph{Rich/lazy: init decides whether even the invariant converges.} With
TransformerLens-default init the invariant itself is still climbing at the end of
\Ptwo's training budget (head-sum cos $0.88$--$0.93$: B2 $0.875$, B $0.930$); rich init
(Brich $0.994$) locks it early. This is
\Pone\ App.-G rich/lazy reproduced in \Ptwo's world: per-head FRA rankings are
checkpoint- and init-dependent even when seeds agree on the canonical parity split.
\htag{measured}

\section{The factored world (Phase 2, \Pone\ App.\ L)}\label{sec:factored}

Phase 2 is \Pone's factored two-Mess3 product
$\Tx=\Tb_1^{(z^{(1)})}\otimes\Tb_2^{(z^{(2)})}$ (9 latent states, 9 tokens,
$d_{\mathrm{model}}=120$, sequence length 11 \emph{with} BOS; \texttt{train\_p1.py}).
The configurations are P1mix2 ($\zeta=(+0.5,-0.5)$, $H=2$), P1pos2 ($(+0.7,+0.4)$,
$H=2$), P1mix3 ($H=3$), 100k steps each. The Phase-1 machinery lifts directly, and the
gauge story becomes richer in three ways.

\subsection{Theory statements}\label{sec:factoredtheory}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Per-factor constrained update.} Each factor $n$ has its own
$\Delta\rb_n=\sum_s\zeta_n^{\,d-s}\gb_n(z_s^{(n)})$ in a ${\approx}$orthogonal 2-d
residual subspace $V_{F_n}$ (\Pone's vary-one identification). The FRA-OV closed forms
(\S\ref{sec:ov}) apply \emph{per subspace}: attribution
$\Ab_{d,s}\Rb_n\vb_s=\zeta_n^{\,d-s}\gb_n(z_s)$, projected by \Pone's \emph{effective
subspace attention} $\alpha_n(d,s)$ --- exactly ``FRA-OV aggregated to the known
subspace $\gb_n$''. The invariant is now the head-sum \emph{per subspace};
per-head-per-factor is gauge. \htag{conjecture, lift of \S\ref{sec:ov}}
\item \emph{Ray reuse and the conic head count.} The negative-$\zeta$ two-ray
requirement (\S\ref{sec:tworealize}) becomes \Pone's conic minimum head count
$H_{\min}$: the fewest heads whose nonnegative cone contains the lag-update set
$\{(\zeta_1^k,\zeta_2^k)\}$. Rays can be \emph{reused across factors} --- e.g.\
$\zeta_1=+0.5,\zeta_2=-0.5$ needs 2 heads, not 3 --- so the per-head gauge family
(Prop.~\ref{prop:hinge}) grows: a head can serve two factors, and $\delta$-freedom now
includes cross-factor re-splits. The head-summed-per-subspace attribution is still the
only loss-pinned object. \htag{conjecture}
\item \emph{The QK gauge survives verbatim.} \Pone's causal shuffles (token-identity
shuffles of QK inputs leave the loss unchanged; lag-breaking shuffles hurt) are exactly
the \S\ref{sec:qk} statement that FRA-QK carries \emph{temporal structure only}; the
tok$\times$tok block is gauge. The G1/$\delta$ dials transfer; the new subspace
structure only adds per-factor pedestals $\bped_n$ and shares $\theta_n$.
\htag{conjecture, lift of \S\ref{sec:qk}}
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Factor-selective interventions and the orthogonal-collateral contrast.}
Because the factor subspaces $V_{F_1}\perp V_{F_2}$ are (approximately) orthogonal ---
unlike the three coplanar $\gb(z)$ of a single Mess3 --- a cut of factor 1's channel
should produce \emph{no} forced collateral on factor 2, in sharp contrast to Mess3's
geometrically forced collateral (\S\ref{sec:collateral}). This is the cleanest
predicted discriminator between the factored and single-process worlds: selective
concept removal is \emph{possible per factor} where it was impossible per token.
The BOS token adds a genuine attention sink (unlike \Ptwo's sink-at-position-1
workaround, Prop.~\ref{prop:sink}), which should \emph{absorb} the normalization warp
and make the per-subspace $\alpha_n$ cleaner than \Ptwo's warped head output.
\htag{conjecture}

The intervention predictions were logged \emph{ex ante} in
\texttt{RESEARCH\_LOG.md} (t${\approx}$2h25) before the battery ran.}

\subsection{Outlook: the two-eigenvalue optimal profile (\Pthree\ lift)}\label{sec:p3lift}
The factored process places \Pthree's optimal-attention analysis in its first genuinely
multi-eigenvalue setting. The product transition $\Tb=\Tb_1\otimes\Tb_2$ has
nonstationary spectrum $\{\zeta_1,\zeta_2,\zeta_1\zeta_2\}$, and the token displacement
carries all three:
$\gb(j)=\gb_1(j_1)\otimes\pib_2+\pib_1\otimes\gb_2(j_2)+\gb_1(j_1)\otimes\gb_2(j_2)$
\htag{exact}. The cross-eigenvalue $\zeta_1\zeta_2$ therefore \emph{enters} the
skip-bigram statistics through the interaction term $\gb_1\otimes\gb_2$ (subdominant,
$\|\gb_1\otimes\gb_2\|\sim\|\gb_2\|/\|\pib_2\|$ times the marginal parts): factor
independence factorizes the beliefs but not the joint displacement, since one token
constrains both factors. \Pthree's force-balance/generating-function route then predicts
an optimal profile that is a sum of \emph{three} geometrics, the in-disc roots of a
degree-$6$ reciprocal polynomial (the generalization of \Pthree's single-eigenvalue
palindromic quadratic; the coupled roots are not in closed form and we compute them
numerically). Per factor the MSE optimum is the \Pthree-shrunk rate
($\eta^\ast=0.60,0.34$ for $\zeta=0.7,0.4$, ratio $0.85$, reproducing \Ptwo's $0.467$ at
$\zeta=0.55$), \emph{but} the shrinkage is loss-flat: using the raw $\zeta$ costs
$2.2\%$/$0.56\%$ of the recoverable lag-information
($6.9\times10^{-5}$/$1.3\times10^{-6}$ MSE). This explains why the trained per-subspace
$\alpha_n$ decays at $\approx\zeta_n$ (measured $0.705/0.381$, \S\ref{sec:factoredmeas})
rather than at $\eta^\ast$: the decay rate is the factored-world instance of the
loss-flat regime of \S\ref{sec:flat}, a near-gauge quantity below the CE gradient floor.
The $\zeta_1\zeta_2$ geometric contributes a real but $\sim\!2\times10^{-5}$ improvement
--- present in the theory, invisible to training.\footnote{Full derivation and
numerics: \texttt{derivations\_p2\_fra.md} \S9 and \texttt{scratchpad/ext\_b.py}.}
\htag{exact spectrum; numerical rates; analytical frontier = coupled roots}

\subsection{Outlook: the mixture ($\lambda\to1$) limit}\label{sec:mixture}
The one regime this sprint deliberately left out is the prior sprint's mixture world ---
a concept carried by a non-decaying ($\lambda\to1$) mode. Every object measured here
extrapolates to that limit. As $\zeta\to1$ the per-lag value ladder of \S\ref{sec:flat}
flattens (rung ratios scale as $\zeta^{2\tau}$), so the loss-flat tail that begins at
lag 3 for $\zeta=0.55$ recedes to infinity: the \emph{entire} profile becomes
loss-pinned, toward the flat optimum of the imported flattening law
(\priorA/\priorB: $1/(1-\eta)\propto(1-\lambda)^{-1/2}$). The $k{=}0$ route question
dissolves --- lag 0 becomes one more equal rung of a counting kernel --- and the FRA
handles inherit their known aggregation-regime roles: the pattern is maximally pinned
yet carries no concept (the normalization pin $\alphatil(1)=1$, \priorB), so FRA-QK
stays gauge for the concept, while FRA-OV aggregation shares --- and hence $c^\ast$ ---

attributed and every measured number is traceable to a named JSON artifact.
