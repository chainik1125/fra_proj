# Hierarchical Gate-HMM Leaky-Reset Process

This note defines the version you described: a hierarchical HMM where the coefficients (`v1`, `v2`) are *latent-state posteriors produced by a gate process*, not fixed equal scalars.

In this construction:
- level 1 gate chooses super-sector (`A` vs `B`);
- level 2 gate chooses child (`1` vs `2`) inside the chosen super-sector;
- each leaf (`A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`) has leaky-reset within-leaf state dynamics.

This is different from the previous 4-block direct-sum note, where prompt dynamics did not move mass between blocks.

---

## 1) State Space

Let:
- `z_t in {A, B}` be the super-sector gate,
- `r_t in {1, 2}` be the child gate,
- `h_t in {0, ..., d_{z_t,r_t}-1}` be the within-leaf hidden state.

Joint hidden state is `(z_t, r_t, h_t)`.

Define leaf masses:

$$
\pi_{zr,t} = P(z_t=z, r_t=r \mid x_{1:t}),
$$

top-level coefficients:

$$
v_{A,t} = P(z_t=A \mid x_{1:t}) = \pi_{A1,t} + \pi_{A2,t},
$$
$$
v_{B,t} = P(z_t=B \mid x_{1:t}) = \pi_{B1,t} + \pi_{B2,t}.
$$

These are the dynamic `v1`, `v2`.

---

## 2) Prompt Dynamics

For prompt token `p_k`, define:

1. Top-gate transition:
$$
K^{(k)}_{\text{top}}(z' \mid z).
$$

2. Child-gate transition (conditioned on destination super-sector):
$$
K^{(k)}_{\text{sub},z'}(r' \mid r).
$$

3. Within-leaf leaky-reset signature `q_{z'r',k}` and strength `lambda_{z'r'}`.

Use:

$$
H^{(k)}_{(zr)\to(z'r')}(h' \mid h)=
\begin{cases}
(1-\lambda_{z'r'})\mathbf{1}[h'=h] + \lambda_{z'r'} q_{z'r',k}(h') & \text{if } (z,r)=(z',r') \\
q_{z'r',k}(h') & \text{if } (z,r)\neq(z',r')
\end{cases}
$$

Interpretation:
- if the gate stays in the same leaf: usual leaky reset;
- if the gate switches leaves: hard reset into the destination leaf signature.

Combined prompt transition:

$$
P(z',r',h' \mid z,r,h,p_k)
=
K^{(k)}_{\text{top}}(z' \mid z)\,
K^{(k)}_{\text{sub},z'}(r' \mid r)\,
H^{(k)}_{(zr)\to(z'r')}(h' \mid h).
$$

Because `K_top` and `K_sub` are nontrivial, prompt tokens move mass across leaves, so `v_A, v_B` evolve over time.

---

## 3) Completion Dynamics

You can use either completion scheme:

- top-tagged tokens (`A`-tagged vs `B`-tagged), analogous to current `leaky_reset`;
- leaf-tagged tokens (`A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`) for stronger hierarchical supervision.

For top-tagged completion token with content index `i`:

$$
w_{zr}(\text{A-tag}) =
\begin{cases}
1 & z=A\\
\beta_{\text{top}} & z=B
\end{cases}
,\quad
w_{zr}(\text{B-tag}) =
\begin{cases}
\beta_{\text{top}} & z=A\\
1 & z=B
\end{cases}
$$

and within each leaf use diagonal readout `D_{zr}(i)` as in current `leaky_reset`.

This preserves the original "tag gives sector evidence" behavior, while now sector masses are already dynamic from prompt gating.

## 4) Worked Two-Level Example

### 4.1 Setup

- Leaves: `A1, A2, B1, B2`
- `d_{zr}=2` for all leaves
- initial masses uniform: `pi_{A1}=pi_{A2}=pi_{B1}=pi_{B2}=0.25`
- initial within-leaf belief uniform: `[0.5, 0.5]`
- prompt write strength for all leaves: `lambda=0.6`
- signatures:
  - for token `p_A`: `q_{zr,A}=[1,0]`
  - for token `p_B`: `q_{zr,B}=[0,1]`

Top-gate transitions:

$$
K^{(A)}_{\text{top}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0.95 & 0.05\\
0.60 & 0.40
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
K^{(B)}_{\text{top}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0.35 & 0.65\\
0.05 & 0.95
\end{bmatrix}
$$

(rows are previous `z`, columns are next `z` in order `[A,B]`).

Child-gate transitions (same for both super-sectors in this toy example):

$$
K^{(A)}_{\text{sub}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0.85 & 0.15\\
0.35 & 0.65
\end{bmatrix},
\quad
K^{(B)}_{\text{sub}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0.30 & 0.70\\
0.10 & 0.90
\end{bmatrix}
$$

(rows previous `r`, columns next `r` in order `[1,2]`).

### 4.2 After prompt token `p_A`

Top coefficients:

$$
v_{A,1}=0.5\cdot0.95 + 0.5\cdot0.60 = 0.775,\quad
v_{B,1}=0.225.
$$

Child conditionals:

$$
\rho_{1|A,1}=0.5\cdot0.85 + 0.5\cdot0.35 = 0.60,\quad
\rho_{2|A,1}=0.40
$$

$$
\rho_{1|B,1}=0.5\cdot0.30 + 0.5\cdot0.10 = 0.20,\quad
\rho_{2|B,1}=0.80.
$$

Leaf masses:

$$
\pi_{A1,1}=0.775\cdot0.60=0.465,\;
\pi_{A2,1}=0.775\cdot0.40=0.310,
$$
$$
\pi_{B1,1}=0.225\cdot0.20=0.045,\;
\pi_{B2,1}=0.225\cdot0.80=0.180.
$$

Within-leaf leaky reset for staying trajectories moves each leaf belief toward `[1,0]`:

$$
\mu'=(1-0.6)[0.5,0.5]+0.6[1,0]=[0.8,0.2].
$$

### 4.3 After next prompt token `p_B`

Top coefficients update again from previous `v_{A,1}=0.775, v_{B,1}=0.225`:

$$
v_{A,2}=0.775\cdot0.35 + 0.225\cdot0.05 = 0.2825,
$$
$$
v_{B,2}=0.7175.
$$

So the `v1/v2` coefficients are explicitly generated by the gate HMM and can swing with token history.

---

## 5) Why This Is Hierarchical (Not Just Direct Sum)

Direct-sum-only model (previous note):
- prompt transition is block diagonal;
- no cross-leaf transitions during prompt;
- top coefficients stay fixed if prompt is neutral.

Gate-HMM hierarchical model (this note):
- prompt transition has cross-leaf terms from `K_top` and `K_sub`;
- mass moves between `A` and `B`, and between children;
- `v_A, v_B` are posterior variables generated by hidden gate dynamics.

Formally:

$$
T_{\text{direct}}(p_k)=\mathrm{diag}(S_{A1,k},S_{A2,k},S_{B1,k},S_{B2,k})
$$

vs

$$
T_{\text{gate}}(p_k)[(zr h)\to(z'r' h')] \propto
K_{\text{top}}^{(k)}(z'|z)\,
K_{\text{sub},z'}^{(k)}(r'|r)\,
H^{(k)}_{(zr)\to(z'r')}(h'|h),
$$

which is generically not block diagonal.

---

## 6) Practical Mapping to Your Earlier `v(P)` Intuition

In this process, for a prompt prefix `P = (p_1,...,p_t)`:

$$
v_1(P)=P(z_t=A\mid P), \quad v_2(P)=P(z_t=B\mid P).
$$

So the old manually chosen/equal prompt coefficients are replaced by coefficients inferred from a latent gate process.
